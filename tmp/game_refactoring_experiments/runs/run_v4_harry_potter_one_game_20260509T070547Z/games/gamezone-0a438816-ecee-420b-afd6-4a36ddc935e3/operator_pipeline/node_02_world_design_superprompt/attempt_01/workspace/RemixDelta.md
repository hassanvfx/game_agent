# Remix Delta

## What the current game appears to be
A neon arcade bug-clearing game where the player drags pests off a central foot-shaped stage to preserve health and survive as long as possible.

## What the remix prompt is asking for
A Harry Potter-flavored transformation that makes the game feel rooted in Hogwarts and wizarding-world magical chaos.

## What should stay stable
- Drag-to-remove controls
- Spawn area behavior
- Distance-based clear logic
- Health drain from ignored threats
- Score and health reward semantics
- Endless escalating arcade loop
- Mobile-safe interaction reliability

## What should shift
- Title and copy
- HUD fiction and labels
- Bug identity into magical pests / pixies
- Stage fiction into a warded magical imprint or castle-defense map
- Background storytelling into a five-world Hogwarts journey
- Color and motif language into magical castle visuals
- Reactions and feedback into wizarding alerts

## What later stages should prioritize first
1. Replace the current identity with a clear Hogwarts pest-control premise.
2. Preserve gameplay readability while redesigning pests and the central stage.
3. Build a procedural magical art system for backgrounds, HUD, particles, and props.
4. Establish five recognizable world beats without changing the mechanics.
5. Keep mobile clarity and drag precision as non-negotiable.

## What must not be overinterpreted
- Do not turn this into a spell-casting combat game.
- Do not add inventories, menus, or character movement.
- Do not rewrite the loop just to force explicit levels.
- Do not reduce playability by making art too ornate or dark.
