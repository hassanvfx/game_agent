# Remix Delta

## What the current game appears to be
The source game is a compact one-screen drag-and-clear arcade loop. Bugs spawn over a large foot-shaped target zone, the player drags them away to remove them, health drains if they stay too long, and score rises with each successful clear. The game already supports start, play, and game-over states, along with a gradual pressure ramp.

## What the remix prompt is asking for
The prompt "Harry Potter" is best interpreted as a Hogwarts-flavored magical containment remix. The game should feel like a castle pest-control emergency or enchanted training drill rather than a generic neon bug-clearing scene.

## What should stay stable
- Drag-to-clear controls
- One-screen survival structure
- Health drain and restore semantics
- Score semantics
- Spawn-driven escalation
- Mobile-friendly interaction model
- Start and game-over flow

## What should shift
- Bugs become magical pests such as Cornish pixies and cursed beetles
- The central target zone becomes a magical footprint ward or containment sigil
- Tweezers become enchanted containment tongs
- HUD labels become ward/shield language
- Menu and failure copy become Hogwarts-themed
- Background identity evolves into five Hogwarts-related worlds
- Particle, glow, and feedback styling become magical rather than neon-club themed

## What later stages should prioritize first
1. Preserve the input and survival loop exactly.
2. Rewrite the fiction and UI copy around magical containment.
3. Replace the visual identity with procedural Hogwarts-inspired art and world progression.
4. Strengthen readability and level differentiation without introducing new mechanics.

## What must not be overinterpreted
- Do not turn the game into spell combat.
- Do not add mana, projectiles, or weapon systems.
- Do not replace drag interaction with tapping or swiping lanes.
- Do not treat the Harry Potter prompt as permission to rewrite core systems.
- Do not sacrifice mobile readability for franchise ornamentation.
