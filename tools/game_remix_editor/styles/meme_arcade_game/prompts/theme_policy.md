# Theme Policy

## Inherited Defaults
- Approved UI font: `Baloo 2`
- Approved display font: `Bangers`

## Direction
Use a meme-arcade reinterpretation with strong gameplay readability.

Core thematic direction:
- chaotic internet-humor energy translated into an intentional arcade presentation
- absurd visual gags and reaction-driven comedy used as feedback punctuation
- playful neon meme culture styling anchored by readable dark surfaces
- intentionally silly tone that still preserves clear hierarchy, onboarding, and play-state legibility
- fast, high-energy viral-joke aesthetics without compromising controls, win/loss semantics, or spatial clarity

Mood targets:
- loud, punchy, celebratory, and unserious
- playful rather than hostile
- high-tempo and exaggerated, but not visually confusing
- socially memetic in flavor without depending on specific copyrighted characters, logos, or unsafe cultural references

Practical guidance:
- prefer bold caption-like framing for titles, splash states, and feedback moments
- prefer sticker, badge, comic-burst, and reaction-face motifs as controlled accents
- prefer neon signal colors over muted prototype palettes
- prefer short, funny, readable feedback language over long diegetic prose
- preserve familiar casual/mobile game readability and strong usability
- preserve clearly separated layers for gameplay space, HUD, overlays, and controls
- when choosing between a better joke and better clarity, choose better clarity

## Theme Constraints
- Keep the game readable in motion and at small mobile sizes.
- Keep gameplay information more visually important than decorative humor.
- Do not use chaos as an excuse for clutter, overlap, flashing overload, or reduced contrast.
- Avoid random meme stuffing; all thematic elements should support a single coherent identity.
- Do not introduce audio or network dependence as part of the theme.
- Do not break boot flow, controls, or player comprehension to deliver a joke.

## Follow-Up
Later milestones should formalize:
- palette references for neon meme-arcade systems
- theme token expectations for captions, badges, overlays, and reaction accents
- compliance checks for branding, font, silent-safe behavior, and network-safe presentation
- how runtime art/game director overrides are decomposed into style-specific spec updates
