# Theme Policy

## Inherited Defaults
- Approved UI font: `Baloo 2`
- Approved display font: `Bangers`

## Direction
Use an original **wizard-school fantasy prototype** direction for this run while preserving mobile-first clarity, playability, and compliance.

Core thematic guidance:
- favor candlelit mystery, enchanted scholarship, house-challenge energy, and youthful magical confidence
- favor familiar casual/mobile readability even when the surface fiction becomes more ornate
- prefer strong atmosphere through symbolic materials, naming, and tone rather than literal franchise copying
- prefer coherent magical-academy presentation over generic dark fantasy clutter
- prefer restrained decorative treatment and clean state communication

## Mood and Atmosphere
The mood should suggest:
- old stone halls
- floating lantern light
- parchment and brass study tools
- observatory-night skies
- magical libraries and lesson chambers
- subtle rivalry, curiosity, and wonder rather than horror or grim violence

Avoid:
- grimdark brutality
- overly gothic illegibility
- chaotic neon “magic”
- jokey parody framing
- direct use of protected franchise terms or copied narrative beats

## Thematic Translation Rules
When adapting gameplay-facing language or presentation:
- reinterpret existing actions as spell practice, charm trials, ward navigation, academy exams, enchanted exploration, or apprentice challenges where appropriate
- reinterpret resources as items such as house points, spell charge, potion stock, ward strength, lesson progress, archive keys, or sigil tokens when clarity is preserved
- reinterpret hazards and obstacles as curses, traps, enchanted creatures, moving wards, spectral interference, unstable charms, or shifting corridors when the original mechanics remain intact
- keep tutorial and instruction language concise, readable, and action-oriented
- use magical-school symbolism as a layer of fiction, not as a reason to change controls or rules

## Palette and Material Expectations
Preferred palette anchors:
- midnight blue
- ink black
- parchment cream
- brass / warm gold
- burgundy accents
- sparing emerald, teal, or violet magical glow

Preferred materials:
- parchment
- polished wood
- brass trim
- engraved plaques
- crests
- rune circles
- lantern glow
- chalkboard or archive-card accents
- celestial / observatory details used with restraint
