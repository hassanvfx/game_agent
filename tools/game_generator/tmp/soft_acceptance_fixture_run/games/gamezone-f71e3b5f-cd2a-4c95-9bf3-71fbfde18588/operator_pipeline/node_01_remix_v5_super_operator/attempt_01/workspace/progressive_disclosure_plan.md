# Progressive Disclosure Plan

## Level 1 proof
Player learns joystick movement, camera view, BODY ARMOR, WANTED stars, and the ENTER DISTRICT flow.

## Level 1.5 proof
Player taps interact to enter/summon a car, switches weapon, rotates camera, and sees the same open-city loop under stronger pursuit framing.

## Five-stage intent
1. Downtown Entry: movement and camera basics in readable neon streets.
2. Ride Control: car entry/summon and driving pressure.
3. Street Trouble: weapon switch and combat feedback.
4. Wanted Heat: stars, sirens, and police pursuit become the pressure language.
5. Overdrive Escape: all systems combine; visual intensity rises but mechanics remain unchanged.

## Withheld/added pressure
Early states keep HUD simple. Later pressure comes from existing wanted/police/collision systems plus stronger feedback, not from extra mechanics.
