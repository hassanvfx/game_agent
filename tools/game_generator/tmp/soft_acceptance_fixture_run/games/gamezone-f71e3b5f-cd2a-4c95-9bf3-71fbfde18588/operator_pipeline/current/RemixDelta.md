# Remix Delta

## Preserved
- Three.js runtime architecture, update loop, joystick/button input, vehicle entry/exit/summon, combat, collision, wanted level, death/restart, camera rotation, and zoom.

## Transformed
- Public identity changed from a generic city chase to Neon Felony: Overdrive District.
- HUD vocabulary changed to BODY ARMOR, WANTED stars, STREET/SIDEARM weapon labels, and district-entry CTAs.
- Procedural materials changed to night asphalt with cyan/magenta lane glow and emissive building windows.
- Lighting/fog changed from daytime city to neon pursuit atmosphere.
- Start/restart copy and floating district brand reinforce the GTA-like open sandbox prompt.

## Safety notes
- All changes are presentational or copy-level except material values and generated texture colors; gameplay state transitions and mechanic equations were not rewritten.
- The preserved source is available as index_old.html for reference-only comparison.
