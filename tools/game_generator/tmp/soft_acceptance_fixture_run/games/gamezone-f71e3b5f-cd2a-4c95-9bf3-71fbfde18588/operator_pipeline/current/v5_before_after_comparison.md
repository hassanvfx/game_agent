## Before
The preserved surface already had a 3D city chase structure with mobile controls, wanted stars, car actions, and combat. Its scene was closer to a daytime/generic city with less authored GTA-night identity.

## After
The canonical surface reads as a neon GTA-style open sandbox: Overdrive District title, WANTED/BODY ARMOR HUD, night-fog scene, emissive road/building textures, compact district badge, and mobile chase controls.

## Mechanics continuity
The same start/play loop, joystick movement, vehicle action, weapon switch, camera rotation, wanted stars, collision and failure paths remain available.

## Transformation judgement
The change is visible beyond title and color because the 3D materials, lighting model, procedural textures, HUD semantics, start copy, and scene mood now point to one authored neon police-pursuit identity.
