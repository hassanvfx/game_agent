# Mechanics Complexity Decomposition

## Protected complexity
- 3D navigation: camera-relative joystick movement and driving.
- Vehicle system: enter, exit, summon, speed, steering, and collision.
- Combat: melee/gun switch, attack cooldowns, damage, and takedown consequences.
- Wanted system: escalation, decay, police spawning, and pursuit pressure.
- Failure: HP/body armor depletion, BUSTED state, restart.

## Presentational complexity
- Neon material system, mobile HUD language, compact GIF/audio feedback, fog/lighting, and procedural city identity.

## Safe escalation knobs
Wanted stars, visual intensity, siren feedback, police presence, and HUD warning tone can escalate without changing mechanics.

## Fake complexity avoided
No new missions, quests, scoring systems, steering model, physics model, or win conditions were invented.
