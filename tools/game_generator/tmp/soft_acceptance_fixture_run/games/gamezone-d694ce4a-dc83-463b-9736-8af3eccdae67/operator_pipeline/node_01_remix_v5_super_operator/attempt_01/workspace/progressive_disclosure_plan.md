# Progressive Disclosure Plan

1. **Level 1 — Idle Boulevard:** player learns intro, HUD, walking, and camera in a readable neon street grid.
2. **Level 1.5 — First Ride:** player learns proximity-based car entry/exit and that shooting is disabled while driving.
3. **Level 2 — Rival Blocks:** rivals teach contact damage and shooting feedback.
4. **Level 3 — Patrol Heat:** HEAT stars clarify wanted escalation and police pressure.
5. **Level 4/5 — City Pressure:** visual intensity and traffic/patrol fantasy rise through signage, glow, and HUD language without changing mechanics.
