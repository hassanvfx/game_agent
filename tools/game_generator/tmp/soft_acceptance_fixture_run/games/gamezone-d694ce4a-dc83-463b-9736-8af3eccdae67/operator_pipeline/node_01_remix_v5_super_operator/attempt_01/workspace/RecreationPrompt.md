# Recreation Prompt

Create **Metro Heat 3D**, a mobile-friendly browser Three.js action sandbox inspired by the prompt “3d gta game.” Preserve the source loop exactly: the player starts from an intro overlay, free-roams a 3D city on foot with WASD/D-pad, enters nearby cars, drives with acceleration/friction/turning, shoots only while on foot, builds a wanted/heat meter, takes health damage from hostile NPCs and police, can toggle camera, and restarts after a health-based failure overlay.

Transform presentation boldly: use a neon urban street setting, a stylized street-runner focal character, underglow cars, police heat stars, palm-city billboards, warm pink/amber danger lighting, arcade HUD panels, and mobile-readable controls. Do not add unsupported missions or scoring; let the existing survival/pursuit mechanics carry the GTA-like fantasy.
