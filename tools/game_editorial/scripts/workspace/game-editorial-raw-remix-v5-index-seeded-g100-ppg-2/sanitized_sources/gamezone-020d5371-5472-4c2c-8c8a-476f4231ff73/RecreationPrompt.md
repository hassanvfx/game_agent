Create a polished single-screen top-down arcade survival game called **Void Slayer: Protocol Omega** with a neon cyber-space presentation. The player controls a small futuristic pilot craft by dragging anywhere on the playfield, and the craft automatically attacks using a glowing orbital blade that rotates around it at a fixed radius. The core loop is survive, position carefully, let the rotating blade slice through incoming enemies, collect dropped data shards for XP, level up, and choose one of three random upgrades before resuming the run. Enemies should spawn endlessly from the edges of the screen, with most acting as direct chasers and a smaller ranged type periodically firing bullets toward the player. Contact with enemies and bullets damages the player hull, and the game ends when health reaches zero, showing final score and reached tier. Include upgrades that improve blade damage, blade reach, movement speed, orbit speed, and max health with a full heal. Present the game with a full-screen canvas arena, holographic HUD showing hull, data score, tier, and XP bar, plus stylish overlays for start, pause, level-up, cheat/admin menu, and game-over states. Use bright cyan, blue, gold, and magenta tones over a dark void background with scanlines, glow, camera shake, floating combat text, particles, strong arcade SFX, looping battle music, and compact reaction callouts. The experience should feel mobile-safe, highly readable, easy to pick up, and tuned for endless score-chasing runs driven by drag movement and automatic rotating melee combat.
