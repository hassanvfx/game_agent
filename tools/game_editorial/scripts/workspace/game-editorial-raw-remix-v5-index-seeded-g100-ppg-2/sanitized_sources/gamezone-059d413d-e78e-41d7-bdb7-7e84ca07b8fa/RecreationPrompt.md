Create a polished, mobile-friendly virtual pet game called **Pocket Pet Star Camp**. The game should open on a bright, cheerful start screen where the player chooses one of three adorable pets: **dog, cat, or hamster**. After choosing, transition into a cozy outdoor camp hub with a pastel sky, drifting clouds, grassy hills, decorative pennant banners, sparkles, and a large cute pet rendered in a bouncy mascot-like style. The visual tone should feel wholesome, playful, colorful, and family-friendly, with oversized rounded UI panels and toy-like feedback.

Build the game around a **hub-and-minigame care loop**. At the top of the screen, show a HUD with a **star counter out of 5**, a **current mode label**, and three stat bars: **hunger**, **hygiene**, and **fun**. At the bottom, place four large touch-friendly buttons for **Feed**, **Shower**, **Race**, and **Sleep**. The player should repeatedly choose activities to improve the pet’s wellbeing and earn stars.

The **Feed** activity should be a short reflex minigame where apples fall from the top of the screen and the player moves the pet horizontally using touch or mouse movement to catch them. Each successful catch should increase the hunger meter. The **Shower** activity should be a cleaning minigame where mud spots appear over the pet and the player rubs, swipes, or moves across them to wash them away, increasing hygiene with each cleaned spot. The **Race** activity should be a simple side-view runner-style challenge where the pet stays near the left side of the screen and the player taps or clicks to jump over incoming rocks; every obstacle successfully cleared should increase the fun meter. The **Sleep** activity should trigger a short dreamy overlay with cozy presentation and feel like a brief rest break rather than a deep subsystem, with a small tradeoff that slightly lowers other stats afterward.

Whenever any stat reaches **100**, award **1 star**, play a celebratory sound/visual reaction, and reset that stat back to around **50** so the player continues rotating through the loop. The overall goal is to earn **5 stars** total, then show a festive win screen celebrating that the pet’s star camp is complete. Include short instructional overlays when entering each minigame, clear feedback sounds for collecting, cleaning, failing, UI taps, and victory, and a forgiving difficulty curve suitable for children and casual players. Support both desktop mouse input and mobile touch input, but present everything as if it were designed primarily for phones and tablets.
