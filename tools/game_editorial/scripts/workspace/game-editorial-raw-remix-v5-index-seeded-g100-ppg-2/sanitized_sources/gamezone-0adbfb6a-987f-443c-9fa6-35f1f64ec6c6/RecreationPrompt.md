Create a polished mobile-first arcade game called **Signal Sprint: Data Drift**. The game should feel like a neon cyber runner blended with a simple 3D platformer. Present everything on a full-screen canvas with a glossy futuristic HUD, animated title card, and clean modal overlays for start, failure, and victory states.

The player controls a small glowing courier signal avatar that looks like a cute cube with a digital face, bright cyan-blue glow, and tiny antenna-like signal marks above its head. The world is an abstract data network suspended in a dark void, with floating rectangular platforms stretching forward into the distance. Use a pseudo-3D camera that follows slightly behind and above the player so the route feels like a forward sprint through cyberspace.

The core gameplay loop should be: begin on a large safe platform, drag on the left side of the screen to steer the avatar with a virtual joystick, tap on the right side to jump, cross a chain of floating platforms, avoid falling, and reach a bright finish platform to stabilize the node. Movement should have momentum, friction, gravity, capped speed, grounded jumping, and satisfying landing behavior so the player must line up jumps and control drift instead of moving on a strict grid.

Structure progression as discrete nodes or levels rather than an endless session. Each cleared node should advance to the next one and regenerate a new course procedurally. Every course should include a safe starting platform, a sequence of normal platforms with varied width, depth, forward gaps, and lateral offsets, and a large finish platform. Make early nodes forgiving with wider landings and shorter jumps. After node 5, shift into a harder “Red Alert” phase with bigger gaps, narrower platforms, longer routes, more sideways drift in the course layout, and stronger danger-colored visuals.

The presentation should be sleek and readable: cyan, blue, and violet highlights in normal stages; red-magenta warning tones in harder stages; glowing grid lines and horizon haze to sell the digital environment; a compact HUD showing the current node and a visible difficulty badge in later stages; and small touch-control hint cards near the bottom of the screen. Include background music plus responsive sound effects for UI clicks, jumping, landing, failing, and clearing a node. Add tiny particle bursts on jump and optional win/fail animated reaction overlays to make the feedback lively.

Keep the tone fast, approachable, and replayable, with instant restarts, simple controls, and a clear skill arc focused on steering, timing, and surviving unstable floating routes through a digital void.
