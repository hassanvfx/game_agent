Recreate a casual web game called **Candy Match 3** as a portrait-oriented, mobile-friendly match-3 puzzle experience with a full-screen candy board and a vivid arcade-candy presentation. The player should use touch or mouse to interact directly with the grid, swapping adjacent candy pieces or making simple drag gestures to line up matches of three or more identical sweets horizontally or vertically. When matches happen, candies should clear immediately, new ones should drop into place, and chain reactions or cascades should be possible so skilled play produces satisfying combo streaks. Structure the gameplay around quick, readable rounds where the player is trying to keep making valid matches, recover from stalled board states, and fulfill score-based or level-style objectives that gradually become harder over time. The controls should feel effortless and responsive on phones, with the board staying central and unobstructed while the surrounding UI remains minimal.

Visually, frame the game with a glowing candy-sky atmosphere: rich purple and pink gradients, sparkles, soft neon highlights, glossy candy colors, and a playful mascot-like tone. Use small external HUD chips or badges to communicate status text such as combo energy, candy collection, or ambience state, but do not let UI overlays block the puzzle grid. Add satisfying audiovisual cues for normal matches, bigger celebratory reactions for combo chains, and a gentle “board jam” or “no good move” response when play stalls. The overall tone should be cheerful, sugary, and arcade-polished rather than realistic or story-heavy. Preserve the identity of a bright confectionery puzzle game for casual players, with progression that feels like classic level-based match-3 design and encourages short replayable sessions with increasingly rewarding cascades.
