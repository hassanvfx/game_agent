Create a polished mobile-friendly browser arcade game called **Sky Courier: Storm Run**. The game should run full-screen in a single canvas and present a bright futuristic storm-sky aesthetic with glowing HUD panels, drifting clouds, strong blue-to-dark gradients, and crisp arcade feedback. The player controls a small hovering courier craft or drone-like vehicle positioned near the lower portion of the screen. The craft moves automatically through an endless aerial route, and the only main control is steering left and right with touch drag or mouse/pointer movement. The controls should feel smooth, forgiving, and easy to use one-handed on mobile.

Build the core gameplay loop as a survival-focused endless runner. The player tries to travel the farthest distance possible while avoiding hazards and collecting helpful pickups. Show a visible distance counter as the main score readout and a health-style meter labeled as a storm buffer or survivability gauge. The run should begin immediately, then steadily get harder as movement speed gradually increases over time. Keep runs short, replayable, and readable within seconds.

Spawn three key gameplay elements from the top of the play space. First, add **energy rings** or similarly themed glowing pickups that restore a meaningful amount of health when collected and produce positive juice such as a bright sound, particles, and a quick celebratory reaction visual. Second, add **cargo barriers** or storm debris that deal heavy damage on collision and create impact feedback. Third, add **lightning strike hazards** that are telegraphed before they activate: show a warning indicator or target zone first, then trigger a fast active strike that damages the player if they remain in its path. This telegraphed hazard is an important signature mechanic and should make the run feel reactive rather than purely random.

Make the environment feel like a forward-moving runner even if the player only moves laterally. Use a scrolling corridor or roadway illusion, center lane striping, atmospheric cloud motion, particles trailing behind the craft, and steadily increasing pace to sell momentum. The craft should hover with a bit of visual bob, glow subtly, and read clearly against the background. The overall style should feel like an accessible arcade remix of endless runner design rather than a realistic flight simulator.

Add strong game-feel polish: looping action background music, clear collect and crash sound effects, low-health warning audio, an optional vibration hook on supported devices, a flashing danger overlay when health gets critically low, and energetic particle explosions for pickups and impacts. Include a clean HUD during play and a stylish game-over overlay when health reaches zero. The game-over screen should show the final distance traveled and a single prominent restart button so the player can instantly jump back in.

Keep the tone **bright, storm-charged, futuristic, and replay-driven**. Prioritize simple controls, readable hazards, escalating speed, and a satisfying dodge-and-collect rhythm where the player survives first, grabs pickups opportunistically, and chases better distance on each new run.
