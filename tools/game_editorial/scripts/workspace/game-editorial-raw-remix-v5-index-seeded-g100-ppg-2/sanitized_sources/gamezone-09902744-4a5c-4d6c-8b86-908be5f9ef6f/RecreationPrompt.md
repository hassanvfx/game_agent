Recreate **Signal Shift** as a polished single-screen browser game: a fast, mobile-friendly neon sci-fi **match-3 score attack** played on a **6-column by 8-row grid**. The player should swap only **orthogonally adjacent** tiles, either by tapping one tile and then a neighboring tile or by dragging from one tile into an adjacent tile. A swap is only valid if it creates a horizontal or vertical line of **3 or more identical tiles**; otherwise the move should animate back and clearly read as a failed link. The whole experience should be readable and satisfying on both desktop and touch screens, with a full-screen canvas presentation and no need for keyboard input.

Build the core gameplay loop around **one-minute runs**. The player’s goal is to score as many points as possible before the timer expires. Show a compact top HUD that frames the score as **SIGNAL** and the remaining time as **UPLINK**, reinforcing a futuristic signal-routing fantasy. Successful matches should remove tiles with a quick fade-and-shrink effect, then make tiles above fall downward and new tiles spawn from above, allowing automatic **cascade chains** to continue resolving until the board is stable. Reward longer matches with stronger multipliers, and make chain reactions increase score further so combo play feels valuable and exciting.

Use **six distinct tile types** with a cyber-cartoon identity, such as a robot, glitch alien, satellite, battery cell, crystal, and electric spark. Each tile should look like a glossy luminous chip with bright gradients, soft glow, and a pulsing inner core, placed on a dark holographic board framed by subtle grid lines. The overall aesthetic should feel like a futuristic arcade console or signal deck: deep navy and purple backgrounds, cyan highlights, warm warning colors, glowing borders, and layered interface polish. Include a selected-tile highlight so the player always understands what is armed for swapping.

Make the feedback especially juicy. Add small toast messages for events like normal clears, combo chains, invalid links, and timeout. For stronger chains, trigger floating **danmaku-style praise text** such as “Signal Surge” or “Neon Sync” drifting across the screen. Include a combo reaction image moment and a fail reaction image moment for emotional punctuation. Audio should include looping ambient arcade background, swap clicks, collection sounds, combo stingers, fail cues, and a game-over sound, with audio unlocking on first interaction. The mood should be **flashy, energetic, playful, and satisfying**, more like an arcade sprint than a calm puzzle session.

When the 60-second timer reaches zero, stop the round immediately, dim the playfield, and show a centered game-over overlay with messaging like **“Signal Lost!”**, the final score, and a simple **tap to restart** instruction. Restarting should instantly begin a fresh run with a newly generated board and the same tight score-chasing loop. Do not add level maps, inventory, story scenes, or complex metagame systems; keep the game focused on short replayable sessions, fast decision-making, bright audiovisual payoff, and clean mobile-safe presentation.
