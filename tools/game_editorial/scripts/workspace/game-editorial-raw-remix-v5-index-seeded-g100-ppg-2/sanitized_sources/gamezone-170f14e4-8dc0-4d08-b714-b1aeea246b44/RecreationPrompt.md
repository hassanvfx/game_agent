Create a mobile-friendly HTML5 canvas game called **Snack Stack Arcade** that feels like a bright retro-futurist arcade toy mixed with a very simple cooking-management loop. Present the whole game on a full-screen dark purple and indigo background with neon cyan, pink, and gold accents, glassy HUD panels, pixel-arcade headline styling, and a playful “space snack machine” tone. The main playfield should be a small **isometric grid of arcade stations** rather than a side view or top-down map, and every station should clearly communicate one of four states: empty, loaded with a new order, actively charging/cooking on a timer, or finished and ready to cash out. The player should interact entirely through taps or clicks, with three large visible mode buttons that define the current action: a **prep/beam-in** mode that places a new snack order on an empty station, a **charge/cook** mode that starts a timed process on a prepared station, and a **jackpot/serve** mode that collects the reward from a completed glowing station. The intended flow must always be easy to read as **beam in → charge → jackpot**, and tapping a tile with the wrong mode should not fail the run, but should produce a playful error response and remind the player of the correct order.

Build the gameplay loop around **endless score chasing** rather than levels or story. Each successful collection should add a fixed amount of money to a visible prize meter, and the player’s skill expression should come from keeping as many stations active as possible at once, minimizing idle cells, and rotating between the three tools efficiently while some stations are waiting on their timers. While a station is charging, show a visible progress bar or glow effect and spawn little steam, spark, or energy particles so the board feels alive. When a station finishes, make it pop visually with a ready-state tray or glowing indicator that strongly invites the player to tap it for cash. The player should be able to understand the game instantly on first touch, so include a tutorial hint panel and a reactive status banner that updates with short arcade-style guidance as tools change and actions succeed or fail.

Make the presentation very juicy and celebratory: every action should trigger satisfying audio and visual feedback, including button sounds, success and failure effects, particles, bounce animation on tiles, floating reward text like `+$25`, a subtle pulse on the score display, looping background music, and optional haptic/vibration hooks for mobile wrappers. The food theme can stay intentionally abstract and stylized—think snack bots, glowing cabinets, prize trays, and synthetic toppings rather than realistic kitchen simulation—but the loop should still clearly resemble a simplified prep/cook/serve workflow. Keep the experience accessible, readable on small screens, and technically lightweight, with no complicated menus, no deep upgrade tree, and no hard lose state; the charm should come from a polished arcade atmosphere, clean tap interactions, and the satisfying rhythm of cycling the whole grid through repeated production turns.
