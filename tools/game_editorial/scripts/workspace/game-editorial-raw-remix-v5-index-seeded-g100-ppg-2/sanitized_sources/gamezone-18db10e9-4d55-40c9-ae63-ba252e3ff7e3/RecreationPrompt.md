Create a polished single-screen arcade cooking game called **Sizzle Chef Rush** where the player runs a busy cartoon grill station using only tap or click controls. The entire experience should be immediate, readable, and built for short replayable sessions on both mobile and desktop.

Place **three frying pans** across the bottom of the screen as the main interactive stations. Near the top, show a **small queue of cartoon customers** entering from the side and lining up to receive the same burger-style grilled item. The core gameplay loop should be: **tap an empty pan to start cooking**, watch the food visually progress from raw to cooked with a **clear circular progress indicator**, wait for an unmistakable **green READY cue**, then **tap the ready pan again to serve** the next waiting customer for points. After reaching the ready state, the food should continue into a burn timer; if the player waits too long, it **burns**, triggers a fail effect, counts as a **miss**, and the pan must be cleared before it can be used again. If the player serves when no customer is waiting, the food should be wasted instead of scored.

Structure the run as a **score-chasing survival session**. Customers should spawn steadily to create pressure, and the player should lose after **3 misses**. The intended skill is managing timing windows across multiple pans at once while also responding to customer demand. There should be no deep inventory, no movement controls, and no restaurant-building meta layer—just fast multitasking, timing judgment, and pressure management.

Present the game with a **bold arcade kitchen aesthetic**: colorful cartoon visuals, exaggerated food states, a clean fullscreen canvas, and prominent UI overlays. Include a **start screen** with simple instructions, an in-game HUD showing **score** and **misses**, and a **game-over screen** with the final score and restart button. Use punchy feedback such as quick particles, small celebratory reactions on successful serves, sharper fail reactions on burned food, and energetic sound effects for starting, ready states, serving, mistakes, blocked actions, and game over. The tone should feel **playful, hectic, and lightly stressful**, like a simplified mobile-friendly blend of casual cooking games and arcade reflex challenges.
