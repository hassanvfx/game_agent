Recreate a polished mobile-friendly browser game called **Quiet Shift: Canine Comfort Drill** as a single-screen arcade stealth-timing experience. The core idea is simple: a cute dog sits in a bright cartoon field, and the player must **hold down with mouse or touch, or press space, to pet and calm the dog**, but must **immediately stop** whenever any patrolling guard turns forward to look. Each guard should cycle through a clear readable pattern: **safe while facing away**, then a brief **warning tell**, then a **looking/detection state** with a forward-facing pose and a visible flashlight or cone of attention. If the player keeps petting while a guard is fully looking, the round fails instantly with a spotted screen, warning sound, and dramatic alert feedback. If the calm meter reaches 100%, the player wins the round, gets celebratory confetti and happy feedback, earns coins based on difficulty, and automatically advances to the next level, where patrol timing becomes harder through faster guards and additional simultaneous guards.

Build the presentation with a playful arcade UI: a start screen with a bold title, three difficulties such as light/standard/lockdown, a visible coin counter, a level label, and glossy glassmorphism panels over a colorful sky-and-grass background. Add a **creator mode** where the player can draw or upload a custom circular portrait-style image for the dog and a separate one for the guard, and save those locally. Also include a **sandbox editor** where the player can place or erase guard positions across the field and then test that custom route using the same stealth-petting rules. Add an optional **chat panel** with a cartoon patrol operator giving short keyword-based responses, plus a small cheat menu with options like ghost mode and turbo comfort. Save coins, unlocks, selected skin, custom uploaded art, and sandbox layouts with local storage. Keep the overall tone **cute, tense, mischievous, and readable**, emphasizing the signature mechanic of **pet only during safe patrol windows**.
