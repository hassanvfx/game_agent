Create a polished mobile-friendly HTML5 arcade game called "Cash Flow Rush" as a single-screen neon finance survival experience. The player controls an expressive wallet mascot on a full-screen canvas using touch, mouse, or pointer input, and the character should glide smoothly toward the tapped or dragged position with soft easing instead of snapping directly. The gameplay loop is simple and immediate: valuable objects fall from the top of the screen, the player moves to catch the good ones, avoids the dangerous ones, and tries to post the highest possible money total before losing all lives. Include three main falling object types with readable silhouettes and strong color coding: regular green cash bills that award a standard score gain, rare gold coins that give a large bonus, and red tax slips or fee cards that damage the player on contact. Start each run with three lives, show a bold futuristic dollar score at the top center, and display remaining lives just beneath it as stylized icons. Increase difficulty continuously by making spawn timing tighter and fall speed faster as the session continues, so the run becomes more intense over time without introducing separate levels. Use a dark futuristic city-night backdrop with subtle animated grid lines, floating currency motifs, cyan and mint glows, and a glossy sci-fi HUD style. Make the wallet hero cute and readable, with a face and subtle bobbing animation, so the tone feels playful rather than realistic. Add satisfying audiovisual feedback throughout the loop: looping background music during active play, distinct sounds for normal pickups, bonus pickups, damage, UI clicks, and game over, plus particles, floating score text, brief celebration and fail overlays, and optional vibration on supported devices. When the player is hit by a hazard, remove one life and show a strong negative reaction; when they collect rewards, celebrate with upbeat feedback. When all lives are gone, stop gameplay and present a clean game-over panel showing the final money total and one restart button that immediately begins a new run. The final result should feel like a short-session score-chasing arcade game with casual mobile controls, flashy feedback, and a humorous money-versus-tax theme.
