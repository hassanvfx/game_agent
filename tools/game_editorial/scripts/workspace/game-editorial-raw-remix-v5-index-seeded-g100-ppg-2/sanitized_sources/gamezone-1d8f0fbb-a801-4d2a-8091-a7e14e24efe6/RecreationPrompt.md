Create a polished mobile-friendly idle/clicker management game called **Embassy Orb Manager DX**. The game should run on a single full-screen screen with a lively animated canvas playfield and a futuristic glassmorphism HUD layered above it. The core fantasy is that the player manages a strange but charming diplomatic agency made of glossy country-themed embassy orbs: each orb is a cute flag-ball mascot with cartoon eyes, a tiny smile, occasional speech bubbles, and bouncing physics inside the screen bounds.

The gameplay loop should focus on **passive credit generation plus light manual tapping**. The player starts with one basic low-income orb and a small amount of money. Every deployed orb automatically earns credits once per second, and tapping/clicking an orb should make it bounce, trigger a small personality reaction, and award a tiny extra credit bonus. This manual input should help at the beginning, but the main long-term progression should come from building a stronger roster of passive earners.

Use a bottom navigation bar with buttons for **Market**, **Roster**, **Regions**, and **Archive**, plus a top HUD that shows current credits, a restock timer, save access, and a pause/settings button. The Market should refresh on a timer and present **four recruitable envoy orbs** at a time, drawn from a weighted rarity pool. Each orb entry should clearly show its name, rarity, cost, and credits-per-second output. When the player buys one, it should either deploy immediately into the active playfield or go into storage if the active roster is already at capacity.

The **Roster** screen should act like a deployment manager. Show how many orbs are actively deployed versus stored, and let the player unequip an active orb to storage or equip a stored orb back onto the field. Include a maximum active-orb cap so roster management matters. The **Archive** should serve as a collectible index showing all possible nation orbs in the game, with locked and unlocked presentation states so players can feel collection progress over time.

Add a **Regions** system with at least two environments: a default route and an unlockable premium route such as a snow or polar route. Unlocking the premium route should cost a lot of credits and provide a visible passive earnings multiplier, around **+15% income**, while also changing the background art and showing a small active-boost indicator in the HUD. This should feel like a midgame progression milestone rather than a cosmetic-only unlock.

Presentation should be bright, readable, and playful: deep blue sci-fi panels, neon cyan highlights, polished glass cards, floating reward numbers, rarity colors, little celebration/blocked feedback moments, and cozy sound design for clicks, purchases, boosts, pauses, and saves. Make it feel responsive on mobile with tap-friendly controls, full-screen canvas interaction, and lightweight haptic-style feedback if available.

The tone should be **lighthearted and collectible rather than serious simulation**. The game should feel like a whimsical countryball-inspired idle manager where players casually check in, buy stronger national envoys, optimize their deployed roster, unlock richer diplomatic routes, and slowly complete a large archive of bouncing flag-ball characters.
