Create a polished single-screen browser game that plays like a two-player Ludo match reimagined as a neon sci-fi orbit race. The player controls a red pilot squad against a blue AI on a centered 15x15 board drawn on canvas, with four tokens per side, a shared outer loop path, separate home bases, player-specific home stretches, and a central finish. Start with a cinematic overlay showing a futuristic title, short mission text, and a start button. During play, show a compact glassmorphism HUD with a large die in the top center, red and blue side labels, and a turn indicator. The human player taps or clicks the die to roll, then taps a highlighted valid red token to move it; the blue side is an automated opponent that rolls and chooses randomly among valid moves. Use core Ludo-like rules: tokens begin in home, a roll of 6 is required to launch a token onto the track, moving onto an opponent’s track space sends that enemy token back home, rolling a 6 grants another turn, entering the final home stretch only happens near the end of the loop, and a side wins by getting all four tokens to the finish. Present everything with a glossy arcade space aesthetic: deep navy starfield background, glowing red and blue route accents, rounded UI cards, futuristic fonts, animated status messages, reaction visuals for captures and blocked turns, and energetic audio for rolling, moving, captures, blocked turns, start, and victory. Keep the match readable, responsive, and touch-friendly, with simple casual AI and an end screen that clearly announces whether the red pilot or blue AI wins.
