Create a full-screen mobile-friendly browser game called **Frontier Collapse** that plays as a top-down survival action experience with a dramatic horror-western / post-collapse presentation. The game should open on a stylish lobby screen where the player enters a callsign and chooses a role: **Settler**, **Ranger**, **Raider**, **Brute**, **Scout Drone**, or an unlockable **Warden** hero. Each role needs clearly different core stats such as health, movement speed, damage, and body size so the choice changes how a run feels. The Warden should begin locked and only become selectable after the player discovers a rare relic during play.

When the player deploys, place them into a large open frontier wasteland rendered on an HTML canvas with simple procedural ground tiles, long-view camera follow, and a readable HUD layered on top. The controls should feel touch-first: a **left virtual joystick** for movement, a **jump button**, and a **single action button** on the right. That action button should do a short-range frontal melee swing or bite depending on the role, so it works both for combat and for harvesting resources. Hitting **trees** should eventually break them and award **wood**; hitting **rocks** should award **stone**; finding and striking the rare **relic/key** should trigger a big celebratory notification and unlock the Warden for future redeploys in the current session.

The main gameplay loop should be: move through the wasteland, harvest supplies, avoid or fight roaming enemies, optionally place structures, and survive as long as possible. Some classes, especially the more human survivor roles, should be able to open a small **build menu** and spend resources to place a **barricade** or a **beacon/flag** near the player. Keep building intentionally light: it should support the survival fantasy without turning the game into a deep crafting sim. Populate the map with simple AI bots, including hostile zombie-raider enemies that wander until they detect the player and then chase them down, dealing damage when close. Friendly or neutral flavor bots can also exist to make the world feel inhabited.

The run should be governed by a visible **survival timer** as well as a health bar. Over time, the player is trying to endure until dusk or until they are killed. Include a small mid-run flavor event where a new crew member or family member joins the player, reinforcing the frontier survival theme. Visually, use strong atmospheric UI styling: a large horror-inspired title in the lobby, futuristic status panels, floating damage numbers, short chat-log style status messages, and occasional animated media overlays for impactful moments like landing a kill or unlocking the relic. Sound design should reinforce every major interaction with menu clicks, melee swings, hits, pickups, build confirmations, damage sounds, game-over audio, and looping background music.

End each run with a dedicated **death screen** that states the reason for failure and shows a **survival score measured in cycles or time survived**. The tone should feel tense, scrappy, and replayable rather than deeply simulation-heavy. The finished game should come across as an accessible arcade survival sandbox where the player keeps redeploying to test different roles, survive longer, gather more efficiently, and chase the power fantasy of unlocking and using the Warden.
