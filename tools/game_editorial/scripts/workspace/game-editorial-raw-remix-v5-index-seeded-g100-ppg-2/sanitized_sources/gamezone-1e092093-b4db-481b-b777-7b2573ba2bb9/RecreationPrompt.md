Create a short mobile-first interactive drama game called **Episode 2: House of Secrets**. Build it as a fullscreen cinematic story experience that feels like a haunted-glam soap opera mixed with a casual visual novel. After tapping, the game should play a sequence of **six pre-rendered fullscreen story scenes** in order, using video clips as the main storytelling device.

The narrative should center on **family conflict, suspicious money, betrayal, accusations, being cast out, and secrets hidden behind the doors of a mansion**. Treat it as an episodic cliffhanger chapter rather than a complete long-form game.

Gameplay should be simple and readable on phones: the player mainly **watches each scene**, then at key pauses gets **two large thumb-friendly choice buttons** at the bottom of the screen. Include **five decision points** total. The choices should be emotionally charged and written like drama prompts such as confronting someone, hearing them out, searching for answers, or calling home. Keep the overall flow **mostly linear** so the selected option changes the emotional framing and player participation more than the major story route.

Use a **dark, polished UI** with near-black backgrounds, violet and magenta highlights, elegant serif title text, sleek sans-serif UI text, and subtle glow effects. Add a compact **HUD** that shows the current scene label and a drama-status badge, plus a **six-dot progress bar** showing episode advancement. Include a **pause/resume button** during scene playback. Add brief **transition flashes** between clips. Reinforce dramatic beats with **short UI sound effects** for start, choosing, reveals, pause, resume, and episode completion, plus a low-volume looping ambient background track while scenes play. For high-emotion moments, briefly show small **reaction GIF overlays** labeled with captions like “Decision made” or “Secret revealed.”

End the experience with an **Episode Complete** screen carrying a mysterious subtitle and a **Replay Episode** button that restarts the game. The overall tone should feel **melodramatic, mysterious, glossy, and bingeable**, like a short interactive mansion-drama visual novel designed for touch devices.
