Create a polished mobile-friendly browser game called **Orbital Hold: Last Beacon** as a single-screen sci-fi survival defense experience. The player should control a **stationary central beacon turret** positioned in the middle of the arena and must hold out against an endless swarm of enemies approaching from all directions. The moment-to-moment interaction should be simple and tactile: on touch devices or desktop, the player presses and drags on the game field to aim the turret, and while the pointer is held down the turret automatically fires a rapid stream of shots toward the current aim direction. Releasing input should stop firing immediately.

Build the presentation around a **full-screen canvas** plus overlay UI. The visual tone should be **neon sci-fi, glassy, high-contrast, and arcade-intense**, with a dark space-like background, glowing grid accents, cyan and gold highlights, scanline flavor, and bold futuristic HUD typography. Include a clear start screen with mission briefing text and a strong game-over panel showing the final kill count. The HUD should always show the current score, the current threat sector or wave, a hull bar for the beacon, and a charge bar that fills as enemies are destroyed. The layout should be touch-safe and responsive, especially for phones.

The core gameplay loop should revolve around three simultaneous pressures: **aiming and shooting**, **protecting the perimeter**, and **deploying special cards**. Enemies spawn off-screen around the outer edges and move inward toward the center. Before they can reach the beacon directly, they should collide with a circular ring of **six perimeter barricade segments** around the center. Active segments absorb attacks and can be broken individually. If a gap opens, enemies that pass through it should reach the center and damage the beacon hull directly. The game ends when hull reaches zero.

Every enemy destroyed should add to the score and also contribute to a **charge meter**. When the charge meter fills, it should generate a random **upgrade card** in a dock at the bottom of the screen, with a maximum of three stored cards at once. These cards must be draggable and should only activate when dropped onto the correct target zone. Cards aimed at the **CENTER** should be dropped near the turret/core. Cards aimed at the **PERIMETER** should be dropped onto the barricade ring. Invalid drops should fail cleanly and leave the card available.

Include these signature cards and preserve their gameplay roles:
- **Field Patch**: restores damaged barricade panels and reactivates destroyed ones.
- **Overdrive**: temporarily increases the turret’s fire rate and gives the weapon a more aggressive visual treatment.
- **Ion Burst**: stuns all active enemies and deals partial damage across the field as an emergency panic ability.
- **Alloy Skin**: upgrades all perimeter segments into stronger reinforced metal barriers with much higher durability.

Progression should be survival-based rather than level-based. Increase pressure over time by scaling enemy health, movement speed, and spawn frequency. Advance the displayed sector or wave every 50 kills so the player feels escalation. Preserve a strong arcade feedback loop with impact particles, muzzle flashes, shell ejections, screen shake, low-health danger vignette, score updates, and energetic sound design for shooting, hits, upgrades, damage, pickups, start, and defeat. The overall experience should feel like a **compact last-stand turret defense game** that combines real-time shooter pressure with light drag-and-drop tactical support management.
