Recreate a polished mobile-friendly browser arcade game called “Chat Rush: Mod Queue.” The game should present the player as a late-night moderator managing a chaotic livestream or community chat feed inside a neon, futuristic, streamer-inspired interface. Use a full-screen canvas for gameplay and overlay it with stylish HUD panels and menu screens. The core loop is simple and fast: short chat messages fall from the top of the screen toward three large action buttons at the bottom, and the player must quickly press the matching button before each message reaches the action line. There are exactly three message categories and three matching actions: toxic or troll posts should be handled with a red “BLOCK” action, supportive fan messages should be handled with a green “BOOST” action, and meme/joke posts should be handled with a yellow “RIDE” action. The player never taps individual messages directly; instead, each button press should automatically resolve against the lowest currently active incoming message, making the game feel like a rapid classification lane challenge. Correct calls should award score, build combo, slightly restore a survival meter, and trigger satisfying audiovisual feedback such as particles, floating score text, a bright success sound, and a celebratory animated reaction image. Wrong calls or missed messages should reset combo, reduce the survival meter, shake the screen, and trigger a failure sound and fail reaction animation. The survival meter should represent chat stability or hype pressure and should also decay slowly over time, forcing constant action even if the player is not making mistakes. As score increases, the game should get harder by making messages arrive or move faster, creating a score-chasing survival structure. Include a strong start screen with the title, a short one-line rules summary, and clear icon hints for the three actions, plus a game-over screen that shows the total cleared posts and offers an instant restart. The visual style should feel like a hybrid of livestream dashboard, Discord-like chat energy, and arcade neon sci-fi, with dark purple-blue gradients, glowing borders, bold futuristic fonts, emoji-like icons, and animated UI polish. Keep the experience readable, punchy, replayable, and easy to play with mouse or touch on both desktop and mobile.
