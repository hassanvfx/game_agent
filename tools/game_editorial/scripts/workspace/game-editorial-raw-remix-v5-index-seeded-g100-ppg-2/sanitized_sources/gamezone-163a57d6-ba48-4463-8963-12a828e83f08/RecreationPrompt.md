Create a responsive single-screen browser game called **Orbital Rescue** with a polished neon sci-fi presentation. The player controls a glowing rescue skiff positioned near the bottom of the screen, moving it horizontally with the mouse or by dragging on touch devices. The skiff should not teleport instantly; it should smoothly follow the target position like a controllable paddle. Above it, a round drifting rescue pod falls under gravity and can bounce off the left wall, right wall, and ceiling with slight energy loss. The core loop is to keep the pod in play for as long as possible: when the pod lands on the skiff, it should bounce sharply upward, gain some horizontal velocity and spin based on where it struck the skiff, and award points.

Design the run as an endless score-attack survival game with no levels, only increasing difficulty through chaotic trajectories and player execution. Each successful rescue should grant **+10 points**, update a visible HUD counter, and trigger satisfying arcade feedback: a bright particle burst, a floating score popup, a subtle screen shake, a cheerful rescue sound, and a short celebratory visual cue or status banner. Missing the pod should immediately end the run once it falls below the bottom of the screen. On failure, play a distinct danger or game-over effect, stop the background music, show a game-over panel with the final score, and offer an immediate retry button so the player can jump right back in.

Present the game with a full-viewport canvas and layered HUD overlays. Use a dark purple-to-indigo space backdrop, drifting starfield ambience, glassy translucent interface panels, futuristic heading typography, and glowing cyan, green, and pink accent colors. Include a start screen with a clear mission pitch, an in-game HUD visible only during play, and a game-over screen with a short retry message. The tone should feel like a stylish arcade rescue mission: tense while the pod is falling, rewarding when the player makes a clean save, and highly replayable in quick sessions. Keep the controls mobile-safe, the layout responsive, and the overall feel accessible, readable, and immediately playable in a browser.
