Create a polished mobile-first browser game called **Neon Vault** that feels like a futuristic relic-draw machine inside a cyberpunk archive chamber. The player should begin with a visible pool of vault funds and interact mainly through one large **SCAN** button. Each scan costs a fixed amount of money, briefly animates a mysterious card or vault core, then reveals a randomized relic card chosen from five rarity tiers. The reveal should feel dramatic: the card shakes, flips in 3D, glows according to rarity, shows unique relic art, and displays the payout value. Common results should be low-value or worthless scrap, while higher tiers should multiply the scan cost into increasingly exciting rewards, with legendary hits feeling like jackpots.

Structure the experience with four clear interfaces: a striking title screen, the main scan screen, an archive/almanac screen, and a full-screen lockout game-over overlay. The visual style should be glossy and high-contrast, using deep indigo and black backgrounds with cyan, violet, magenta, and gold highlights. Add a subtle animated grid or particle background, translucent glass panels, bold futuristic typography, and strong rarity-based color coding. The overall tone should be slick, suspenseful, and celebratory, like a sci-fi slot machine crossed with a collectible codex.

The gameplay loop should be simple and addictive: spend funds to scan, reveal a relic, apply its payout multiplier to the balance, permanently mark that relic tier as discovered in an archive, then immediately invite the next scan. The archive should show all possible relic entries, with locked placeholders for undiscovered ones and full art/name display for unlocked ones. Preserve tension by allowing bad luck to drain the player’s funds; if the balance drops too low to continue, trigger a dramatic lockout screen with failure audio and a restart button that restores the starting bankroll for a new run.

Controls should be direct and tap-friendly with no character movement or complex systems. Focus on responsive button presses, strong audiovisual feedback, and very short session loops. Include background music, UI click sounds, reveal sounds, stronger bonus and jackpot cues for better rarities, and a harsher fail/game-over sound for lockout. Make rare and legendary reveals especially memorable using larger particle bursts, brighter glows, or reaction media. The signature feeling should be **anticipation, reveal spectacle, bankroll risk, and archive completion** in a compact, replayable, one-button collectible game.
