Create a polished mobile-friendly arcade game called **Slipstream Neon** as a single-screen top-down survival runner set on a futuristic three-lane highway at night. The player should control a glowing cyan interceptor-like vehicle on a full-screen canvas and steer it horizontally by dragging a finger, touching, or moving the mouse left and right across the road. The core loop should be simple and immediate: start a run, dodge incoming hostile traffic spawning from the top of the screen, survive as long as possible, and chase a higher distance score shown in meters. The road should feel like a neon corridor with bright lane markers, glowing shoulders, moving dashed lane dividers, and a vivid cyberpunk skyline in the distance, with stars and soft light blooms animating in the background even when the game is idle.

Build a clean presentation layer over the canvas with a stylish start screen, an in-game HUD, and a game-over screen. The start screen should introduce the fantasy of piloting a radiant interceptor through dangerous midnight traffic, while the HUD should show the current distance and include a sound toggle. On failure, show a dramatic crash-themed results panel with the final distance and a fast restart option. Keep the game highly replayable with instant restart flow and no menus beyond what is needed to begin and replay.

Gameplay should revolve around avoidance only: enemy vehicles or rogue drones appear in random lanes and move downward toward the player, and any collision ends the run immediately. The score should rise continuously based on survival distance, and difficulty should ramp over time by increasing movement speed and shortening the interval between obstacle spawns. Movement should feel smooth and reactive, with the player ship/car easing toward the latest pointer position and visually tilting slightly while steering. Preserve strong silhouette contrast so the player vehicle is always bright cyan and enemies read as warmer, more dangerous colors like pink, orange, violet, or gold.

The tone should be sleek, tense, energetic, and arcadey rather than realistic. Use glowing futuristic typography, glassy UI panels, punchy sound effects for start, pass, crash, and mute toggle, plus looping background action music during active runs. Add brief visual feedback for success and failure, such as celebratory pass flashes or a crash animation/GIF overlay, to reinforce the rhythm of near-misses and sudden run-ending mistakes. Keep the design compact, readable on phones, and centered on one signature mechanic: fast lane-switching survival in a neon sci-fi corridor.
