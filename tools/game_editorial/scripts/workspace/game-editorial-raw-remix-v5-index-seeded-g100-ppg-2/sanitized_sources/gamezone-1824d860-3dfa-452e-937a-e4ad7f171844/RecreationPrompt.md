Create a polished browser-based neon arcade space shooter called **Neon Blast**. Build it as a fullscreen HTML5 canvas experience with a vivid sci-fi presentation: glowing cyan, pink, and gold highlights; a moving starfield; soft nebula gradients; subtle light streaks; and glassy overlay panels for the start screen, HUD, and game-over screen. The game should feel like a fast, replayable score-attack arcade title rather than a deep progression game.

The player controls a small starfighter that sits near the bottom of the screen and can move **only horizontally**. On desktop, movement should follow the mouse across the canvas; on mobile, it should follow finger drag/touch movement smoothly and remain responsive on narrow screens. The ship should **fire automatically** upward at a steady rhythm, so the core skill is positioning: the player dodges incoming threats while lining up the ship so its auto-fired shots destroy them.

Spawn colorful meteor-like or diamond-shaped hostile space objects from random positions above the screen and make them descend toward the player. Give them slight variation in size, color, and speed so the field feels alive. When a bullet hits one, remove it immediately, spawn a bright particle burst, play satisfying hit/explosion audio, briefly flash an animated hit reaction, and increase the score. If any falling object collides with the player ship, end the run instantly, stop the background music, trigger a failure sound and failure reaction visual, and show a game-over panel with the final score plus a one-tap restart button.

Structure the gameplay loop around **instant readability and fast retries**: title screen, launch button, active run, collision-based game over, instant reboot. There should be no inventory, no upgrades, no dialogue, and no level map—just endless survival with difficulty ramping over time through more frequent or faster enemy spawns. Include a small HUD that clearly shows score and uses a clean arcade style. The overall mood should be flashy, accessible, energetic, and mobile-safe, with the sensation of piloting a glowing ace fighter through a neon danger corridor for “one more run” score chasing.
