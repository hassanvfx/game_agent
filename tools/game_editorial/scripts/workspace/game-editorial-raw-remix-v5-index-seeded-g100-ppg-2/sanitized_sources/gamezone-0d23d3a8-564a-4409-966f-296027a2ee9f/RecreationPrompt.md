Create a polished, full-screen mobile-friendly browser game called **“Signal Sprint: Night Shift Chat”** that feels like a futuristic neon messenger interface layered over a live signal grid. The main fantasy is that the player is working a calm cyber night shift, keeping a relay channel active while guiding a glowing courier across the field. Use a fullscreen canvas as the visual backdrop and place a sleek chat UI over it: a sci-fi header with avatar and subtitle, a scrolling message feed, a soft-glow status chip, an expandable text input, and a circular send button. The visual tone should be dark, blue-violet, and high-tech, with subtle grid motion, halo glows, soft glassmorphism panels, and readable mobile-safe spacing.

The core gameplay loop should be continuous and low-pressure. The player can **type messages** to a fictional “guide” character and receive delayed replies that **stream in character by character**, creating the illusion of a live relay conversation. The guide’s responses should feel lightly roleplayed and atmospheric, sometimes using italicized action narration like scene direction. The player can also **tap or click anywhere on the background field** to reroute a glowing courier avatar toward that location. The courier should move smoothly toward the selected target, idle gently when stationary, and occasionally wander on its own if left alone for a while. Add small particle effects or icon bursts when certain chat prompts are recognized and when a route is locked in.

Presentation and feedback are important. Include ambient looping background music, light UI sound effects for send/typing/route confirmation/error, and optional vibration or haptic feedback on supported devices. Add small floating visual confirmation panels for route changes and channel activity, and update a compact status readout with phrases like standby, live, relay typing, relay clear, or route locked. The experience should not have combat, fail states, score, timers, puzzles, or level progression. Instead, progression should be atmospheric and experiential: send a message, watch the guide answer, redirect the courier, enjoy the audio-visual response, and repeat. The final result should feel like a cozy cyberpunk communication toy with a messenger-app surface and a living neon field underneath.
