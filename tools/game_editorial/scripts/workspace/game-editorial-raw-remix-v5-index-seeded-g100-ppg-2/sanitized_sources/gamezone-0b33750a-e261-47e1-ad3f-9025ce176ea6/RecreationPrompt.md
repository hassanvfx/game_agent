Create a polished full-screen browser arcade game called **Arcade Appetite: Day 99**. The game should play as a single-screen neon survival challenge where a cute circular mascot sits near the bottom of the screen and can only move horizontally, following the player’s mouse on desktop or finger drag on mobile. From the top of the screen, a steady stream of falling items should appear: most are tasty food pickups such as burgers, pizza, fries, noodles, donuts, hot dogs, and other snack-themed icons, while a smaller but dangerous set of hazard items should be visually distinct and cause immediate failure on contact.

The main gameplay loop should be simple, readable, and fast: slide left or right to catch food, avoid hazards, and survive long enough to raise a visible **Day** counter from **1 to 99**. Every food pickup should also increase a **Weight** stat and slightly enlarge the player avatar so the character feels like it is bulking up during the run. That growth should be rewarding visually but also make survival harder by increasing the collision size over time. The game should end instantly if the player touches any hazard, showing a game-over panel with the final day reached. If the player reaches Day 99, trigger a flashy victory celebration with explosive particles, triumphant audio, and a celebratory overlay.

Use a strong arcade presentation: glowing purple and pink sci-fi gradients, floating decorative particles, big comic-style title text, bright HUD pills for Day and Weight, and juicy feedback for every meaningful event. Add a start screen that clearly explains the objective, a retry flow for failed runs, and a replay button after victory. During active play, include a looping background music track and distinct sound effects for starting, eating food, streak bonuses, danger moments, failure, and victory. Streak milestones should feel exciting, with combo reactions, brief animated visual feedback, and light screen shake when food is caught. Keep the tone playful, meme-like, and over-the-top, as if the player is surviving an absurd buffet gauntlet.

The final result should feel like a compact, mobile-friendly arcade minigame focused on reflexes, readability, and escalating tension: no exploration, no deep story, just dodge, collect, grow, survive, and celebrate when the impossible Day 99 run is completed.
