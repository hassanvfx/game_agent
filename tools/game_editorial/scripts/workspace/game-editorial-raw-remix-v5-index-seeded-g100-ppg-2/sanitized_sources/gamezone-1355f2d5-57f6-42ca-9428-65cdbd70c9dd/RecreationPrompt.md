Create a mobile-friendly single-screen arcade game called **DM Rush: Influencer Edition** where the player moderates an influencer’s incoming live-feed messages in real time. The game should feel like a fast casual reflex challenge with playful social-media satire. Use a full-screen portrait-friendly layout with a dark purple-to-indigo gradient background, subtle glowing grid accents, glossy glassmorphism HUD panels, and bold futuristic typography. The presentation should evoke a neon cyberpop streamer dashboard rather than a realistic simulation.

The core gameplay loop is an endless survival score chase. Chat-message bubbles descend from the top of the screen toward the bottom. Each bubble is either a **supportive fan message** or a **hostile hater message**. The player never taps the bubbles directly. Instead, the entire screen is split into two input zones: tapping or clicking the **left half** performs **LOVE**, and tapping or clicking the **right half** performs **BLOCK**. Each input always applies to the **lowest active bubble on screen**, making the game about urgent classification and timing rather than free aiming.

Design the message bubbles as chunky rounded speech cards with strong color coding and iconography. Fan messages should use pink/red tones, heart-like symbols, and upbeat short phrases like “First like!”, “You’re trending!”, or “Keep posting!” Hater messages should use green toxic/warning tones, harsher symbols, and dismissive phrases like “Spam alert,” “Bad take,” or “Hard pass.” Fan bubbles should appear slightly more often than hater bubbles. The player must quickly decide whether the most urgent bubble deserves support or rejection before it falls off-screen.

On a **correct** response, remove the bubble, award score points shown as **Verified Fans**, play a satisfying positive sound, show a brief celebratory reaction popup, and spawn emoji-like particles near the bubble. Correct responses should also increase an internal difficulty multiplier a little so the run becomes more intense over time. On an **incorrect** response, remove the bubble but reduce a life meter displayed as a percentage, play a fail sound, trigger stronger negative reaction feedback, and add screen shake or vibration. If the player simply lets a bubble fall off the bottom of the screen, that should also reduce life and count as a failure. Add a low-life warning cue when health gets dangerously low.

Progression should be endless rather than level-based. Difficulty should ramp through faster effective bubble movement and a steadily accelerating spawn rate. The player’s goal is to survive as long as possible and maximize the Verified Fans score before life reaches zero. When life is depleted, show a polished game-over overlay with wording that frames the failure as the stream/feed being shut down because too many bad messages slipped through. Display the final fan count prominently and include a large immediate **Restart Stream** button that resets everything into a fresh run.

Include juicy feedback and mobile-first polish: looping background music during play, distinct score/fail/warning/restart sound effects, lightweight GIF-style reaction cards for success and failure, optional device vibration when available, and responsive sizing that works cleanly on phones. Preserve the signature identity of the game: a binary left-versus-right moderation mechanic, a constant focus on the lowest falling bubble, escalating arcade pressure, and a humorous influencer-themed presentation.
