Create a mobile-friendly browser game called **Meme Runway Studio** that feels like a flashy neon fashion mini-game with memphis-style UI, bold display typography, bright pink/cyan/yellow accents, and a playful runway-show mood. The core experience should begin on a full-screen canvas where the player uses touch or mouse input to trace dashed clothing guides. Provide a small palette of vivid colors, a Clear button, and a Done button. The interaction should feel simple and immediate: tap a color, draw directly on the silhouette, clear if needed, then lock in the round with Done.

Structure the game as a short three-part progression. In **Round 1**, the player traces a meme-inspired outfit shape. In **Round 2**, the player traces a viral dress shape. After each round, reveal a **Next** button so progression feels explicit and arcade-like. Do not overcomplicate the drawing system with scoring, physics, or precision grading; the tracing phase should mainly serve as a creative, guided fashion activity.

After the two drawing rounds, transition into a **fashion trivia quiz** presented as a centered overlay card above the same stylized background. Ask **three multiple-choice questions** about clothing or color basics, advance immediately after each answer, and keep a visible “question X of 3” progress indicator. Track the number of correct answers as the main score the player finishes with.

Use lively presentation feedback throughout: upbeat background music for the drawing phase, a different loop for the quiz phase, and short click, success, fail, and win sound effects. Show a small reaction image or animated card for positive and negative outcomes, such as runway hype for success and a style oops reaction for mistakes. If available, support light vibration feedback on mobile-like platforms.

End on a game-over screen that celebrates completion with a fun title such as **Meme Diploma!**, shows the final quiz score, and includes a replay button labeled in a remix/retry style such as **Remix Again**. Keep the whole game lightweight, cheerful, easy to read on phones, and centered on this loop: **trace the fashion template, progress through two rounds, answer three style questions, view the score, and replay**.
