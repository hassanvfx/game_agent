Create a mobile-first browser game called **Karma School Loop: Notebook Edition** as a compact, choice-driven school-life time-loop experience with a playful hand-drawn notebook presentation. The game should run in a single-screen vertical layout designed for touch, with large buttons, oversized emoji scene art, bold comic-style headings, status bars, and a cozy doodled paper aesthetic that shifts into a darker night palette by evening. The tone should mix wholesome teen social comedy, low-stakes rebellion, awkward romance, and exaggerated consequences.

The core gameplay loop is a repeating single day made of **exactly 8 actions**. Structure the timeline around slots like **Wake Up, Commute, Homeroom, Classes, Lunch, Afternoon, Clubs, and Evening**. Each scene should show a large emoji, a scene title, a short funny or expressive description, and **2 to 4 tap-friendly choices**. Picking a choice should usually consume the current action and advance to the next time slot. At the end of the 8th action, trigger a sleep/reset transition, increase the day number, clear temporary location and wanted-state conditions, nudge karma slightly back toward neutral, and start the next loop from morning again.

Include a visible **karma bar** running from BAD to GOOD, a **day counter**, **time display**, **current action counter**, and status icons for things like being **injured** or **wanted by police**. Choices should modify a karma value in both positive and negative directions. Helpful, thoughtful, or socially kind actions should increase karma. rude, criminal, or selfish actions should lower it. Keep the writing short, punchy, and humorous, with choices that feel expressive rather than mechanically dense.

Build a **notebook side menu** with three tabs: **People**, **Acts**, and **Map**. The **People** tab should list characters the player has met, each with an emoji portrait and a relationship percentage bar. Discovered characters should include school-life archetypes such as a crush, bully, teacher, janitor, bestie, mom, dad, ghost, and stray dog. Selecting a person should open a modal with interaction types like **Chat**, **Compliment**, **Prank**, and **Fight**. If a character’s relationship reaches a threshold such as **50%**, unlock a special **Ask Out** option. Social interactions should use short randomized conversational outcomes that affect relationship values and sometimes karma.

The **Acts** tab should provide side-activity categories like **Crime**, **Social**, and **Health**, each with multiple actions and randomized outcomes. Crime actions should raise **crime heat**, which acts as a danger meter. Some criminal outcomes should trigger **instant arrest**, while repeated crimes should guarantee arrest after enough heat accumulates. Social actions can boost general bonds or produce embarrassing failures. Health actions can improve karma and reinforce the feeling of managing the day beyond just dialogue scenes.

The **Map** tab should let the player visit locations such as **School**, **Home**, **Store**, and **Doctor**. Visiting a place should switch the scenario pool so the player continues drawing scenes from that location until they manually leave. The Doctor location should be important because fights can cause an **injured** state that disables some physical actions until the player is treated. If the player gets arrested, the rest of the day should shift into a **jail mini-loop** with special scenes and choices, visually reinforced with a jail-bar overlay and slightly heavier consequences.

Presentation should reinforce every decision with quick feedback: floating text toasts, expressive sound cues, and occasional sticker-like reaction animations or GIF-style overlays for positive social wins or chaotic failures. Keep all interaction direct and simple—no free movement, no complex inventory, no precision gameplay. The charm should come from replaying short days, meeting people, pushing relationships upward or downward, flirting with disaster, and seeing how a mix of good choices, bad impulses, school drama, and loop repetition reshapes the day.
