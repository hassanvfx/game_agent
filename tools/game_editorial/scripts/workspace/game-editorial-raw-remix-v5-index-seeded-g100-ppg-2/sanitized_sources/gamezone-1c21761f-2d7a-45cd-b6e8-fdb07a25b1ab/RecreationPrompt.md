Create a polished mobile-friendly browser game called **Cosmic Foundry**. The experience should be a single-screen, full-viewport idle/incremental space game with a dark neon sci-fi presentation, a drifting starfield background, glowing HUD panels, retro-futuristic typography, and a calm ambient audio loop. Begin with an attractive title screen that fills the screen and invites the player to tap anywhere to ignite the forge. Once started, reveal a compact HUD showing current credits and passive income per second, plus a floating planet-themed button that opens a bottom-sheet or side-sheet shop called a star catalog.

The core gameplay loop should be simple and satisfying: the player starts with a small amount of money, opens the catalog, buys the cheapest available celestial body, waits while passive income increases automatically every second, and reinvests those earnings into more expensive and more powerful space objects. Progression should move through a long ladder of purchasable cosmic entities such as asteroids, dwarf planets, planets, giants, dwarf stars, black holes, galaxies, and finally a universe-scale end-tier item. Every purchase must do two things immediately: increase the player's passive income and add a new visible object into the canvas scene so the space backdrop gradually transforms into a living collection of drifting celestial bodies.

Controls should work cleanly on touch and desktop: tap or click to start, tap the floating shop button to open and close the catalog, and tap purchase buttons for items. Optional taps on the main canvas can trigger small particle bursts for tactile feel, but the game should not depend on fast action, aim, combat, or platforming. This is an open-ended progression toy, not a skill challenge. There should be no fail state and no heavy narrative, but the game should communicate a fantasy of building a beacon-lit cosmic production line that grows from a tiny rock into a universe-spanning idle empire.

Presentation details matter: use glossy glassmorphism-style UI panels, bright cyan/blue/violet accents, glowing celestial objects, and smooth motion in the background. Shop entries should show an icon, the celestial object's name, its cost, and how much passive income it adds. Purchase buttons should disable automatically when the player cannot afford an item. Successful starts and purchases should feel rewarding with celebratory visual feedback, subtle particles, and pleasant audio cues, while failed purchase attempts should trigger a softer blocked response. The overall mood should feel relaxing, luminous, futuristic, and quietly awe-inspiring, with the joy coming from watching the economy scale upward while the starfield becomes denser and more majestic.
