Create a polished mobile-first browser game called **Life Sprint**, a single-screen arcade life simulator where the player manages one character’s entire adulthood through fast yearly choices. Start the run at age 18 with visible stats for **age, cash, health, morale, skills, and current career**, and make every button press advance exactly **one year**. The core loop should always offer four large touch-friendly actions: **Work/Shift** to earn money but reduce morale and health, **Study/Train** to raise skills but spend money, **Relax/Recover** to restore morale and a small amount of health, and **Socialize/Network** to improve morale at a money cost. Add a small baseline yearly drain so each turn naturally wears the player down even before the chosen action applies, making burnout prevention a constant concern.

Build progression around a **career ladder** that unlocks better-paying jobs when the player’s skill stat reaches thresholds, with promotions feeling exciting and slightly uncertain rather than fully automatic. Higher careers should dramatically improve earnings, creating a strong incentive to invest in learning even while survival pressure is rising. Include **random yearly life events** after some turns, such as surprise bonuses, fees, audits, commute costs, burnout hits, morale boosts, or other short life moments that can help or hurt the run. End the game if **health reaches zero**, framing it as collapse from exhaustion or burnout, and also include a positive long-run ending if the player survives to **age 100**.

Presentation should feel like a **neon sci-fi arcade dashboard** rather than a realistic office sim: dark futuristic gradients, glowing glass HUD cards, animated bars, large readable typography, and a central avatar that visually changes with age and condition. Show a compact event log with the most recent important moments, floating feedback text for gains and losses like money or skill increases, and strong positive/negative reaction overlays for good and bad outcomes. Add energetic UI sound effects for taps, wins, warnings, promotions, and game over, plus looping background music that can be toggled on or off. Keep the whole experience lightweight, replayable, and satisfying, with simple controls, clear tradeoffs, and a tone that feels fun and colorful on the surface while still communicating the pressure of work-life balance and long-term burnout management.
