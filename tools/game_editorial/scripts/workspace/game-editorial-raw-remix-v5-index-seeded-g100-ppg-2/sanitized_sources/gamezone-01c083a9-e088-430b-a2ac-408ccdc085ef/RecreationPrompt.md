Recreate a browser-based game called **Hex Siege**, a fast turn-based abstract strategy duel played on a small **radius-3 hexagonal board** with a flashy neon sci-fi presentation. The player controls the **Red Clan** and faces an AI-controlled **Iron Clan** in a self-contained one-match tactical showdown. Each side starts with **6 circular clan pieces** placed on opposite edges of the board. The player interacts entirely with **tap/click controls**: select one of your own pieces, view the highlighted legal destinations, and tap an empty hex to commit the move. Most pieces can move exactly **1 hex in any of the 6 directions**, but a piece occupying the exact **center hex** gains a special mobility bonus and can slide farther in straight hex lines until blocked or until the board edge. Enforce a key signature rule: **a move is illegal if it ends with 3 allied pieces aligned in a straight row**, so the game should clearly reject those moves and show a short feedback message.

After every move, resolve combat automatically by checking every piece on the board at once: **any piece adjacent to 3 or more enemy pieces is removed**, which allows simultaneous losses on both sides. The player’s goal is to use spacing, traps, and encirclement to cut the Iron Clan down to **2 pieces or fewer** before the AI does the same to the Red Clan. Include a simple but competent AI that scans all legal moves, strongly favors moves that create captures, strongly avoids suicidal placements where it would immediately be surrounded, and mildly values moving toward more central positions. Structure the match as a repeating loop of **player turn → move → surround-resolution → AI turn → move → surround-resolution** until one side wins, then show a restartable game-over screen.

Present everything in a **fullscreen canvas** with a glowing futuristic battlefield: dark starry background, luminous circular board sockets, glossy orb-like clan pieces with subtle personality, animated movement easing, and a HUD that displays both remaining piece counts plus the current turn. Add a **start screen**, a **sound toggle**, and a **game-over overlay** with win/lose messaging. Use short **toast notifications** for invalid selections or blocked moves. Give the game a polished arcade feel with looping ambient background music, crisp UI and capture sound effects, and small celebratory/defeat animated reaction overlays for captures and match endings. The overall tone should feel **tense, elegant, readable, competitive, and immediately replayable**, like a minimalist board tactic game wrapped in high-energy mobile-friendly arcade presentation.
