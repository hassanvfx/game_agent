Create a polished mobile-first neon sci-fi arcade game called 'Signal Sprint: Stay Online' where a small robot courier runs endlessly from left to right across floating platforms in a digital sky. The game should play like an endless runner and platformer hybrid: the player holds a large on-screen BOOST button to accelerate forward, releases to lose speed through friction, and taps a large JUMP button to perform a single grounded jump. Use simple physics with gravity, horizontal acceleration, friction, and top-surface platform collision so the core challenge is timing jumps across randomized gaps while preserving enough momentum to stay alive. Generate platforms endlessly ahead of the player with varied widths, gaps, and moderate height shifts, keep the camera following horizontally, and score the run by distance traveled, presented as increasing signal bars. Include a start screen with a cute glowing robot mascot and short instructions, an in-game HUD showing ONLINE status and score, and a game-over overlay that says the signal was lost and offers an instant reconnect restart. Present everything with a glossy cyber aesthetic: dark blue space-like gradients, cyan and magenta glow, futuristic fonts, a rounded robot avatar, subtle particles, celebratory milestone feedback, fail reactions, looping background music, and jump, land, score, UI, and fail sound effects. The tone should feel lively, accessible, and replayable, emphasizing quick retries, score chasing, and staying in the flow.
