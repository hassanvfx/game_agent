Create a responsive single-screen browser arcade game titled “Neon Defense Grid.” Present it as a futuristic cyber-defense experience with a full-screen canvas, a glowing sci-fi HUD, animated particle ambience, and a subtle neon grid background. Before gameplay, show a polished start panel with a short premise about protecting a sentinel core and a bright call-to-action button. During play, spawn moving circular breach markers across the screen at regular intervals. Make two target types: a common cyan target that is larger, slower, and worth 10 points, and a rarer amber target that is smaller, faster, and worth 50 points. Give every target a visible countdown ring so the player can tell when it is about to expire. The player should interact only by clicking with a mouse or tapping on touch devices directly on the targets. If the player hits a target, remove it instantly, award points, play a satisfying hit sound, and show a small burst explosion effect. If the player misses anywhere on the playfield, subtract one life immediately. Also subtract one life whenever a target expires naturally. Start with three lives displayed clearly as hearts in the HUD alongside the score. Increase difficulty gradually by spawning targets more frequently, increasing their movement intensity after successful hits, and raising the chance of fast amber targets as the score increases. Add warning feedback when the player is down to one life and end the run with a dramatic game-over panel showing the final score and a restart button. Use energetic audio, bright cyan/amber/crimson feedback colors, and crisp arcade-style presentation so the experience feels like a mobile-friendly neon score-attack defense game built around precision, speed, and survival under pressure.
