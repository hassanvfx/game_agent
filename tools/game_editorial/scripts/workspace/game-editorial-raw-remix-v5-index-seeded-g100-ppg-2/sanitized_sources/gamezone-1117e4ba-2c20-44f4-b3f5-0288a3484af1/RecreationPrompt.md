Create a mobile-first 3D arcade action game set on a neon cyber court where the player controls a glowing courier from a third-person elevated camera view. Use a virtual joystick on the lower left for movement and a single large action button on the lower right that normally makes the character jump, but changes into a dunk or jack-in command when the player is carrying the objective and is close enough to the hoop. Start each run with the character near one end of the arena, a floating glowing data core spawned somewhere ahead on the floor, and a few roaming drone hazards already moving around the court. The gameplay loop should be simple and replayable: move to collect the data core, carry it safely through the arena, reach the glowing hoop or uplink near the far end, trigger a dramatic slam-dunk style score action, then immediately spawn another core and continue the run. Each successful dunk should award 1 point and periodically increase difficulty by adding more drones, so the game becomes a score-chasing survival challenge over time. A collision with a drone should instantly end the run and show a polished game-over overlay with final score and retry/main-menu options. The tone should feel stylish, fast, arcade-like, and slightly tense rather than realistic. Present it with strong cyberpunk cues: dark void background, cyan/orange/pink neon accents, holographic backboard, glowing court lines, pulsing halo effects around the hoop, animated collectible bobbing, reactive particle bursts on scoring, and clean HUD prompts that switch between instructions like securing the core and reaching the uplink. Include a start screen, pause overlay, and game-over screen, plus snappy audio cues for start, jump, pickup, dunk, pause, resume, blocked attempts, landing, and failure. The camera should smoothly follow the player during gameplay and orbit the arena during the start screen showcase. Keep the mechanics intentionally accessible and arcade-driven rather than simulation-heavy: movement, jumping, pickup proximity, dunk range checks, hazard avoidance, score escalation, and immediate replayability are the signature constraints.
