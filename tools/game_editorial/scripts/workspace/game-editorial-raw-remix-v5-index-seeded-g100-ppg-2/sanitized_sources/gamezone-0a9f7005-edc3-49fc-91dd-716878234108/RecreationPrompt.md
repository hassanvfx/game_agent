Create a mobile-first HTML5 game experience called **ArcadeHub Mobile** that looks like a polished neon arcade social app in portrait orientation. The overall presentation should feel like a futuristic mobile lobby with a top header, a coin counter, a profile chip, and a four-button bottom navigation bar for **Home**, **Profile**, **Crew**, and **Setup**. Use a glossy sci-fi palette with deep purple panels, dark backgrounds, cyan and violet accents, warm yellow highlights, rounded cards, soft glows, and energetic arcade styling.

The **Home** tab should present the game as a social arcade hub. Feature a large banner for the flagship game **Sky Sprint** with copy that makes it sound like a one-thumb arcade challenge, plus two smaller quick-challenge cards for **Snack Catch** and **Signal Duel**. Include a lightweight friends rail with stylized avatars and an overall vibe similar to a playful online game platform. The **Profile** tab should show a player avatar, name, short bio, and a couple of simple stats. The **Crew** tab should show a short message list that can open a mock chat thread. The **Setup** tab should include simple toggles for music and sound effects. These social screens are mostly flavor and framing, but they are important to the identity of the experience.

When the player taps any game card, open a **full-screen gameplay overlay** above the app shell. This overlay should include a compact HUD at the top with a score display labeled as **XP**, a visible close button, a centered hint like “Tap to Start” or a game-specific instruction, and a game-over modal with the final score plus **Try Again** and **Main Menu** buttons. The whole design should remain highly readable and touch-friendly on phones.

The flagship game, **Sky Sprint**, should be the strongest part of the experience and define the game’s main identity. Build it as a simple endless runner / platform survival game on an HTML5 canvas. The player controls a small colorful mascot runner with a simple chunky silhouette. The character should stay near the left side of the screen while the world scrolls from right to left. Gravity pulls the character downward, the player can **tap to jump only when grounded**, and floating platforms are generated ahead with random widths and heights. The challenge is to keep landing safely as the scroll speed gradually increases. Place collectible coins above some platforms and award bonus points when they are collected. If the player falls below the screen, the run ends immediately.

Add a second mini-game called **Snack Catch**. This one should be a fast casual catcher game where a chef avatar sits near the bottom of the screen and moves left or right depending on which side of the screen the player is touching. Items fall from the top at increasing speed. Most of the falling items should be pizzas worth points, while some should be bombs that instantly end the run if caught. Keep the rules extremely easy to understand and focused on quick retries.

Add a third mini-game called **Signal Duel**. This should be a reaction-timing duel on a minimal dramatic screen. At first the game says to wait. After a random delay, a clear cue such as **TAP NOW!** appears, and the player must tap quickly during a short reaction window. If the player taps too early, show a failure message and end the round. If the player waits too long after the cue, also fail. A successful tap should add to score, briefly show positive feedback, then restart the duel with a shorter wait or tighter timing window so the pressure builds.

Keep the progression structure **session-based rather than persistent**. The game should feel like an arcade toy made for fast bursts: launch a challenge, react quickly, chase a better score, fail fast, retry instantly. Preserve the sense that the app is a mini arcade collection, but make **Sky Sprint** feel like the featured main attraction.

Use responsive touch controls throughout and support mouse input as a desktop fallback. Add playful audiovisual feedback: menu clicks, jump sounds, collect sounds, fail/win cues, menu music in the lobby, more action-oriented music during gameplay, and small success/failure reaction animations or GIF-like overlays that do not block controls. The tone should stay bright, energetic, casual, and replayable, with an identity somewhere between a mobile arcade launcher, a mini-game collection, and a social game portal.
