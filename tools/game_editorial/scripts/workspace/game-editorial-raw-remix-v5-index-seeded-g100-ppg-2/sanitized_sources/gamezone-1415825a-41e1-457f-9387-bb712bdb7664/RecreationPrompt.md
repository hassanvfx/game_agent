Create a polished mobile-friendly browser game called **Stadium Juggle Challenge**: a single-screen arcade soccer juggling experience set in a dramatic night stadium under floodlights. The entire game should play on one full-screen view with a large central soccer ball rendered on a canvas, stylish overlay UI, and a fast restart loop built for short repeat sessions.

The player interaction must be direct and simple: on desktop, click the ball; on mobile, tap the ball. Each successful contact should kick the ball upward, add one point to the juggle counter, and slightly change the ball’s horizontal drift and spin depending on where the player touched it. Off-center hits should matter, so players can correct the ball left or right while trying to maintain a rhythm. The ball should feel responsive and readable rather than overly realistic: gravity constantly pulls it down, wall bounces are allowed with some damping, and the run ends immediately if the ball touches the ground.

Structure the game as three clear states: a stylish start screen, the active gameplay state, and a game-over screen with the final juggle count plus a retry button. During active play, show a compact HUD with the live score and a streak/combo message area. Every few successful juggles, trigger a celebratory milestone message, a bonus sound, and a quick visual reaction to make longer streaks feel exciting. On a miss, stop the action instantly, play a fail cue, show a “streak over” style result panel, and let the player restart immediately.

The presentation should feel like an energetic sports broadcast mixed with a neon arcade aesthetic: deep blue night sky, subtle mist or drifting clouds, a glowing floodlight wash, dark crowd silhouettes, vivid green turf, field markings, a soft spotlight lane behind the ball, and a glossy ball with shadow, shine, and visible rotation. Add particle bursts on successful kicks, slick translucent HUD panels, bold sporty typography, and small celebratory/failure reaction visuals to emphasize momentum.

Include audio that supports the arcade loop: looping background music during gameplay, a punchy kick sound on each hit, a combo reward sound for streak milestones, a start/restart UI click, and a fail sound on game over. Keep the design focused on score chasing rather than levels or narrative. The signature experience should be pure reflex-based freestyle juggling: keep the ball airborne, stabilize its drift, build a streak, lose everything on a drop, and jump straight back in for another attempt.
