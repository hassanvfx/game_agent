Recreate a compact, polished mobile-first arcade game called **Neon Shatter**. The game should play on a single fullscreen screen with a dark futuristic neon look, using a glowing cyber-grid background, cyan and pink lighting accents, gold bonus highlights, and red danger cues. The presentation should feel like a premium hyper-casual arcade score chaser with strong juice and immediate readability.

The player does not move a character. Instead, the entire game is controlled through direct **tap/click input** on falling targets. Geometric neon targets spawn from the top of the screen and drift downward while rotating. The player must rapidly tap the correct ones before they reach the bottom edge. Standard cyan targets are the main score source and should award **10 points** when hit. Rarer gold bonus targets should be visually distinct and award **50 points** when hit. Red hazard targets should be clearly threatening and must **not** be tapped; hitting one costs a life instantly.

Give the player **3 lives**. Missing any safe cyan target or gold bonus target by letting it fall off the bottom should also cost a life. Hazard targets may pass off-screen without penalty. The game should end immediately when lives reach zero, then show a dramatic game-over overlay with the final score and a prominent **Retry** button that restarts the run cleanly.

Build the progression as an **endless score-attack loop** rather than level-based play. At the start, targets should spawn at a manageable pace, but the spawn interval should gradually shorten over time so the game becomes more intense and demands faster recognition and reaction speed. Randomize target size, horizontal position, fall speed, and rotation so the field stays lively and slightly chaotic.

Make the feedback a major part of the experience. Successful hits should trigger bright particle bursts, floating score text, satisfying sound effects, and a little screen shake. Bonus hits should feel extra rewarding. Mistakes should trigger a harsher fail cue, stronger feedback, and visible life loss. Include looping background music during play and distinct sounds for hit, bonus, fail, button click, and game over. If supported, use light haptics for normal hits and stronger haptics for damage. Small animated reaction cards or popups for events like direct hits, bonus grabs, breaches, and total failure should reinforce the arcade spectacle.

Use a readable HUD pinned over the playfield that shows **Score** and **Lives** at all times. The game must feel responsive and mobile-safe: full-viewport canvas, safe-area-aware overlay UI, touch-friendly controls, and layouts that still read clearly on narrow phone screens. The overall mood should be **fast, flashy, tense, and satisfying**, with the core loop centered on recognizing good vs. bad falling targets under rising pressure and surviving as long as possible for a high score.
