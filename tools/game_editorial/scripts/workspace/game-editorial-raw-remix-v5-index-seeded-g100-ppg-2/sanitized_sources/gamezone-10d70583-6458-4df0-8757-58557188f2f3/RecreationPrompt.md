Create a mobile-first browser game called **Mind Stone Sequence** that plays like a sleek sci-fi memory puzzle on a single full-screen canvas. The core loop should be: show the player a short ordered row of unique numbered tiles near the top of the screen, give them a brief memorize phase with a visible countdown bar, then shuffle those same tiles into a lower pool and ask the player to rebuild the original order by tapping them back into empty ghost slots above. Input should be simple and touch-friendly: tapping a tile in the pool places it into the next open slot, while tapping a tile already placed in the top row sends it back to the pool so the player can correct mistakes before the row is full. Once all slots are filled, automatically verify the answer after a short “verifying” beat.

Structure the game as a wave-based survival score chase. Start with a small sequence length and gradually increase difficulty over multiple waves until the sequence caps at around six unique digits. Award score for each successful reconstruction, with later waves giving higher rewards. A single incorrect full reconstruction should end the run and bring up a game-over overlay showing the final score and a prominent restart or “reboot” button. The moment-to-moment play should feel fast, readable, and replayable, emphasizing short-term recall and quick visual scanning rather than dragging or complex controls.

Present everything with a polished holographic terminal aesthetic: deep dark background, subtle grid lines, neon blue/violet/gold highlights, futuristic HUD panels, and labels like **DATA**, **WAVE**, **SCAN THE CODE**, **REBUILD THE ORDER**, and **SIGNAL LOST**. Use smooth tile interpolation, ghost slot outlines, small particle bursts on success, shake or disruption feedback on failure, and satisfying audio/haptic-style response for clicks, success, blocked actions, and mistakes. The mood should be focused, sleek, and lightly tense, like decoding a digital signal under pressure. Keep the interface responsive for portrait phones, preserve safe-area spacing, and make the entire experience feel like a compact arcade memory challenge with strong visual clarity and immediate replay value.
