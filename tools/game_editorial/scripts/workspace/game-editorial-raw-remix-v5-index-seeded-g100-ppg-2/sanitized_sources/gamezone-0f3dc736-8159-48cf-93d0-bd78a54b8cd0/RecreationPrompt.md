Create a polished single-screen browser arcade puzzle game called **Sky Signal Relay**. The player should control a launcher positioned at the bottom center of the screen and clear a suspended field of colored signal orbs by **dragging to aim and releasing to fire upward**. The core loop should closely resemble a classic bubble-shooter structure: shots can rebound off the left and right walls, the fired orb snaps into the nearest open slot on a staggered hex-style grid, and any connected group of **3 or more matching colors** pops immediately. After every successful attachment or pop, detect any clusters that are no longer connected to the top row and make them fall away for bonus points.

Build the round as a **score-driven survival puzzle**. Start with several partially filled rows near the top of the board, keep the player cycling through one orb at a time, and create tension by ending the run when newly placed orbs reach a danger band near the bottom of the visible field. If the player completely clears the board, immediately begin a fresh round instead of ending the session so the experience feels endless and arcade-like.

Use a **futuristic aurora relay** theme for the presentation. The background should feel like a dark night sky or high-altitude skyline with soft gradients, subtle atmospheric glow, and cyan-blue HUD accents. The orb pieces should look luminous and glossy, with clearly different colors, soft bloom, and satisfying pop effects. Frame the fiction as a beacon network under storm interference: the start screen should present the game like a relay mission, and the fail state should feel like the signal network was overwhelmed.

Controls must be very readable and work for both **mouse and touch**. Show a visible aiming guide while the player is dragging, keep the HUD simple with a prominent score readout, include a start screen and a game-over overlay with final score and retry button, and provide a mute toggle for audio. Add responsive audiovisual feedback: looping background music, a distinct firing sound, pop and combo sounds, warning cues when the stack gets low, small particle bursts when orbs clear, and occasional celebratory or danger animations to emphasize combo moments and near-failure states.

Keep the overall tone **bright, reactive, satisfying, and lightly tense**, with clean arcade readability, mobile-safe layout spacing, and a clear emphasis on chaining pops, dropping disconnected clusters, and chasing a higher score over repeated runs.
