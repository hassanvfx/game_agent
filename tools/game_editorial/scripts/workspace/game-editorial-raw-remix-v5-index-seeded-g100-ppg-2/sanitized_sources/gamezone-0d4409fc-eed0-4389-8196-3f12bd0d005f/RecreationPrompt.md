Create a short, polished mobile-first top-down exploration game called **Snack Scout: Neon Hall Snack Dash** set inside a neon-lit anime-styled school full of anthropomorphic student characters. The player should begin on a start screen that clearly explains the mission: collect **five lunches** hidden across the campus and complete the run. Present the game like a cheerful arcade scavenger hunt rather than a deep RPG, but give it enough personality that the school feels alive.

Build the world as a vertically extended tile-based school interior with a bright central corridor and rows of classrooms on both sides. Each classroom should have a door opening from the hallway and include readable obstacles such as tables, chairs, lockers, and chalkboards. Movement should happen through a large touch-and-drag virtual joystick designed for phones and tablets, with no need for keyboard controls. The player should be able to move freely in all directions while the camera follows smoothly across a map larger than the viewport. Collisions should make walls, lockers, tables, and boards feel solid, while doors remain traversable and chairs can be used as navigable interior clutter.

Use a pixel-art or faux-pixel visual style with strong neon blues, purples, pinks, and cyan glows. The HUD should be simple and readable: show the current active character name, a large collection counter like **0/5**, a sound toggle button, and a character-switch button. Include four playable scout characters with distinct looks and names but identical abilities. Tapping the switch button should cycle between them instantly, update the displayed name, and trigger a short flavor quote so the cast feels expressive without changing the mechanical balance.

Scatter lunch pickups randomly around valid walkable locations in the school, especially in hallways and classrooms, but keep them away from the initial spawn area so the player must explore. Picking up a lunch should happen automatically on contact, increase the counter, play a satisfying sound, emit a few celebratory particles, and briefly show a fun reaction overlay or sticker-like animation. Populate the school with many NPC students standing in rooms; when the player walks near one, a dialogue bubble should appear automatically with a short line of ambient campus chatter or a soft hint about the snack run. These NPCs should add atmosphere and direction, not create branching quests.

The gameplay loop should be simple and replayable: start the run, sweep the glowing hallway, duck into classrooms, avoid obstacles, collect lunches, hear quick bits of character dialogue, and finish as soon as the fifth lunch is secured. As soon as all five lunches are collected, stop active play, trigger upbeat completion feedback, and show a win screen with a celebratory message and a replay button that resets the map state for another short run. Keep the tone cute, energetic, friendly, and accessible, with presentation cues that blend school adventure, snack collecting, and light social charm.
