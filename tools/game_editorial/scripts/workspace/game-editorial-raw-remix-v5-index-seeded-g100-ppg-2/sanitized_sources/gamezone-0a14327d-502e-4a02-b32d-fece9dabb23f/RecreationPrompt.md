Create a polished mobile-first browser game called "Pocket Clinic: Calm Care," a casual vertical clinic-management tower builder presented on a full-screen canvas with a glossy futuristic HUD and modal interface layered above it. The player begins with a reception/check-in room, a small amount of starting cash, and one prominent build button for opening new clinic rooms. Each purchased clinic should appear higher in the tower, alternating left and right around a central shaft so the structure visibly grows upward over time. Patients should spawn automatically at the bottom, walk into reception, then be assigned to any available room of the correct type. Treatment should happen automatically over a timer, after which patients leave and award currency. The player never directly controls characters; the game is driven by tapping buttons to build, tapping placed rooms to open a modal, and buying per-room upgrades for treatment speed and payout value. Include smooth drag-based vertical scrolling so the player can browse the growing tower on touch devices.

Make the progression economy central to the loop: room costs should scale upward, upgrades should get more expensive each level, and the effective player level should correspond to total rooms owned. Around level 25, introduce a new patient subtype tied to mental wellness. These special patients should have a sanity meter that decays while they are waiting or untreated, changing state from green to yellow to red. Yellow-state patients should try to reroute into a specialized calm-care room if one exists. Around level 28, unlock a second build button that lets the player open padded calm suites dedicated to stabilizing these patients. If a mental-health patient reaches red state, they should become a hazard instead of a standard customer: they stop following the normal queue flow, chase another nearby patient, trigger a dramatic impact effect on contact, and then both characters are removed as a soft penalty representing a breakdown in care. Calm suites should prevent this by restoring unstable patients and still awarding money when treatment completes.

Use a clean, colorful presentation with cyan glass HUD panels, bold all-caps button labels, floating reward text particles, upbeat clinic iconography, and a bright gradient sky behind the building. Include money, level, and next-unlock information in the HUD, plus a room-upgrade modal that pauses active simulation updates while open. Add responsive touch-friendly controls, subtle vibration support when available, looped background music, distinct sound effects for clicks, money, success, failure, modal open/close, and danger events, plus brief animated badges or stickers that flash when care succeeds or an alert incident happens. The tone should feel warm, approachable, and satisfying like a lightweight mobile tycoon, but with a signature triage twist where the player must balance clinic growth, room efficiency, and emotional stabilization to keep the tower calm.
