Create a polished browser-based endless runner called **Clinic Dash** set inside a glowing futuristic hospital corridor. The player should control a cute pink mascot-like patient or helper who automatically runs from left to right in spirit while the world scrolls toward them from the right. The only active control should be **jump**, available through tap, click, Space, or Arrow Up, making the game feel immediate, mobile-friendly, and one-button accessible.

Build the gameplay loop around **survival plus collection**. Hazard characters should spawn from the right and move left across the screen at varying heights and sizes. Use a mix of cartoon animal threats such as ants, bears, leopards, eagles, and bats so the player has to read whether to stay grounded or jump. Alongside those hazards, spawn collectible **bandages** that act as the score item. Touching a bandage should increase the score, trigger upbeat collection feedback, and encourage risky but rewarding jump timing. Touching any hazard should immediately end the run.

Use simple but satisfying jump physics with gravity, grounded landing, and a readable floor plane. The player should not manually move horizontally; the challenge comes from timing jumps against incoming objects. As the run continues, gradually increase scroll speed to create escalating tension and make later survival harder. The overall progression should be endless and arcade-based rather than level-based, with the score representing how many bandages were collected before failure.

Present the game with a **full-screen canvas** for the action and lightweight overlay UI panels. Include a stylish start screen with the title, a short premise about collecting bandages for **Poppy**, and a clear start button. During play, show a compact score HUD in one corner. On failure, show a game-over panel with the final score and a restart button. The visual style should feel glossy, neon, and playful: cool blue corridor lighting, sci-fi clinic architecture, glowing accents, and a strong contrast between the cute main character and the incoming hazards.

Add strong arcade feedback. Include background music plus separate sound effects for starting, jumping, landing, collecting, reaching small milestones, and failing. When the player collects a bandage, show floating positive text callouts such as quick medical encouragement lines. Small animated reaction panels or visual pop-ups for successful pickups and failure states should reinforce the game feel without blocking the play area. Keep the tone energetic, cute, slightly surreal, and highly replayable, with the central fantasy being: survive the corridor, gather medical supplies for Poppy, and beat your previous high score.
