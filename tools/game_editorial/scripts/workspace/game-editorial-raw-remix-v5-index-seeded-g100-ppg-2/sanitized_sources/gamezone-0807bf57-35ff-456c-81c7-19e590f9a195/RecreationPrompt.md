Create a mobile-friendly browser game called "Neon Courier 3D" presented as a compact cyberpunk arcade experience in a glowing 3D city-grid plaza. The player controls a futuristic courier runner shown as a simple stylized 3D character with a glowing visor, backpack, boosters, and a pulsing ring underfoot. Before starting, show a polished neon start card with the title, a short arcade-delivery subtitle, and four selectable suit colors that only change the player’s appearance. When the player starts, switch to a HUD that shows a delivery score plus buttons to toggle visual quality and camera mode. The core gameplay loop should be free movement around a bounded square arena using WASD on desktop and a thumb-stick style virtual joystick on mobile. Populate the arena with about fifteen roaming courier NPCs that wander between random targets and have floating glowing markers so they are easy to spot. When the player moves close to an NPC, count it as a successful delivery sync or drop-off, increment the score, play upbeat success sounds, show a toast message and a celebratory animated overlay, and briefly display a chat bubble over that NPC with delivery-flavored text. Let NPCs become scoreable again after the player leaves their radius so the session can continue indefinitely as a score chase. Include a neon shader-style floor or glowing lane grid, atmospheric lights and fog, subtle object bobbing and pulsing emissive effects, looping background music, and responsive UI that remains touch-safe on phones. Offer two camera views: an isometric chase-like view and a higher top-down view. The overall tone should feel sleek, energetic, readable, and casual rather than difficult, with no combat, timer, or fail state.
