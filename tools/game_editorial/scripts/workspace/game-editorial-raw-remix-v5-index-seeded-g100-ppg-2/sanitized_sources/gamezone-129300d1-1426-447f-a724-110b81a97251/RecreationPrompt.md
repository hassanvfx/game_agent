Create a polished single-screen arcade defense shooter called **Sky Shield** set above a glowing neon skyline at night. The player should feel like they are operating a stationary futuristic defense platform called the Prism Sentinel rather than moving a character around the field. The core interaction is simple and immediate: the player aims with the mouse or a touch point, sees a luminous crosshair follow that position, and taps or clicks anywhere on the playfield to fire instant-hit shots. Build the game for both desktop and mobile play, with touch-friendly controls and a clean full-screen presentation.

The gameplay loop should revolve around hostile breach drones rising into view from below the screen, entering the defended airspace, hovering briefly, and then escaping if the player does not destroy them in time. Each escaped drone should cost one shield or life, and the run should end after three losses. Reward every destroyed target with score and structure the entire experience as an endless survival score-attack game with quick restarts and strong replayability. Difficulty should ramp naturally by increasing spawn pressure, enemy toughness, and enemy speed as the score rises so the game becomes more demanding the longer the player survives.

Include three switchable weapons displayed as large, readable buttons along the bottom of the screen. The first should be a balanced rapid-fire pulse weapon for reliable general use. The second should be a wider burst or scatter weapon that trades precision for spread coverage. The third should be a heavy precision lance that fires more slowly but deals very high damage. Weapon choice should matter moment to moment, encouraging the player to swap depending on target placement, urgency, and toughness. Show the currently selected weapon clearly and reflect its identity through distinct reticle color or shot feedback.

Visually, present everything on a full-screen canvas with a dark sci-fi atmosphere: a starfield background, a faint aurora or glow in the sky, and a subtle perspective grid suggesting a defended city barrier or futuristic rooftop line. Enemies should read as hovering stylized drones with bright colors, clear silhouettes, and visible health bars. Firing should produce immediate audiovisual reward through tracer flashes, small impact bursts, larger destruction particles, and strong hit confirmation. Add a reactive aiming reticle with a visible cooldown indicator so the player can read weapon timing at a glance.

Wrap the action in a sleek UI. Start with a stylish title screen that introduces the premise of defending the skyline shield, then transition into a compact in-game HUD showing score and remaining shields. On failure, show a game-over panel with the final score and a prominent restart button for instant replay. The overall tone should be energetic, polished, and cyber-arcade, using bright cyan, magenta, blue, and gold accents against deep navy and black backgrounds. Favor clarity, responsiveness, and short-session intensity over narrative complexity, exploration, or progression systems like shops and upgrades.
