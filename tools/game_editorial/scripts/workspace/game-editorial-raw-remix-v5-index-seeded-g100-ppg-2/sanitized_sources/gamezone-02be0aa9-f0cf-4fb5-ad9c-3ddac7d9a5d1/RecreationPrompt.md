# Recreation Prompt

Create a polished single-screen browser game called **Street Hoops** that feels like a neon arcade streetball score-attack experience. Set it on a late-night outdoor blacktop court with a glowing futuristic city skyline, glassy UI panels, bold arcade typography, and a colorful neon palette dominated by orange, cyan, pink, and deep indigo. The entire game should run in a full-screen responsive canvas layout with lightweight overlay UI for the title screen, score HUD, and game-over panel.

The core gameplay loop should be simple and highly replayable: the player starts each run with one basketball resting near the lower-left area of the court and uses **mouse or touch drag-and-release controls** to aim and shoot. While the player is dragging, show a visible aiming line and a dotted trajectory preview that helps estimate the arc. On release, launch the ball using the inverse drag direction and scale the force by drag distance, with a maximum power cap so the shot remains controllable.

Use **arcade-style physics**, not full realism. The ball should travel with gravity, bounce off side walls with dampened velocity, bounce and skid on the floor with friction, and interact with a simple backboard collision zone. Place the hoop on the right side of the screen and randomize its horizontal position and height after every successful basket so each new shot requires quick adjustment. Count a basket only when the ball is moving downward and crosses through the rim bounds, so scoring feels tied to a believable swish moment.

Each made shot should feel rewarding: increment the score immediately, update a compact HUD, play a celebratory sound, flash a quick reaction visual or animated overlay, emit a burst of particles near the hoop, and then reset the ball for the next attempt after a short pause. A miss should not instantly end the run; instead, let the shot fully resolve with bounces and rolling until the ball comes to rest. Once the missed shot settles, end the run, stop the active music, play a failure sound, show a game-over overlay with the final basket count, and offer a replay button that restarts the score chase immediately.

The tone should be **fast, stylish, and addictive**, with short runs that encourage “one more try” repetition. Include responsive support for both desktop and mobile, preserve clear readability on small screens, and make the presentation feel flashy but lightweight. Important signature elements to preserve are the **drag-to-shoot interaction**, **trajectory preview**, **randomized hoop between successful shots**, **single-ball score streak structure**, **neon nighttime streetball atmosphere**, and **strong audiovisual feedback for makes and misses**.
