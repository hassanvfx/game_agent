Create a casual mobile-first arcade cooking game called **Midnight Street Chef** set at a colorful neon night market. Show the action from a front-facing view of a small street-food stall, with a chef behind the counter, a glowing city skyline in the background, and a sky that shifts from warm sunset colors into a starry night scene. The core interaction should revolve around one big touch-friendly **Tap to Cook** button near the bottom center of the screen. Every tap should fill a cooking progress meter, and when that meter completes it should produce one serving of food stock that becomes visible on the counter.

Customers should enter automatically from the left side of the screen, walk to the stall, line up briefly, and wait to be served. If food stock is available, they should automatically buy after a short pause, increasing the player's cash total. If stock is empty and they wait too long, they should leave unhappy with a negative feedback cue. Make the player's main goal to keep tapping fast enough to maintain stock, satisfy demand, and earn the highest possible amount of money in an endless arcade loop.

Include a simple HUD that clearly shows **cash**, **time of day**, and **current stock**. Add a looping day-night cycle that is not just cosmetic: when night arrives, show a visible **Night Bonus x2** indicator and double the sale value of each serving. The game should feel readable, punchy, and rewarding, with floating cash text, steam particles when cooking finishes, coin-like burst effects on sales, cheerful sound effects for cooking and selling, a fail sound when customers leave, and a light looping background music track.

Visually, aim for a cozy but energetic neon-street-food mood with saturated oranges, purples, pinks, and gold highlights, plus soft glow effects on lanterns, the moon or sun, and the HUD panels. Use playful arcade-style presentation rather than realism. Keep the design lightweight, touch-friendly, and immediate, with no complicated menus or deep simulation systems. The signature feel should be a rhythmic loop of **tap to cook, build stock, auto-serve customers, catch the night bonus, and chase a higher cash score forever**.
