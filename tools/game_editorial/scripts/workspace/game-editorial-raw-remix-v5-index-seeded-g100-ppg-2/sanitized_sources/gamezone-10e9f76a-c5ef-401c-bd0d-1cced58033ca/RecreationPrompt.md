Create a polished full-screen browser arcade game called **"Impostor Run: Swarm"** in which the player controls a small impostor-like astronaut inside a neon sci-fi space-station arena by moving the mouse on desktop or dragging on touch devices. The character should not snap directly to input; instead, it should smoothly drift toward the current pointer position, creating slippery evasive movement that feels good for dodging danger. The core gameplay loop is an endless top-down survival score attack: one glowing vent pickup is active at a time, the player must reach and collect it, the score increases, a new vent immediately appears somewhere else in the arena, and enemy pressure rises after every successful pickup. Enemies should be colorful crewmate-style patrol units that spawn from the screen edges, move on straight vector paths, bounce off arena boundaries, and instantly end the run if they touch the player. Structure the experience with a stylish start screen, a visible in-run HUD showing current vents swept, a short onboarding hint such as **"Drag to Drift,"** and a game-over screen that displays the final score and offers a restart button. Present everything with a dark futuristic palette, glowing cyan and magenta accents, subtle moving grid lines, drifting starfield particles, glassy interface panels, and juicy feedback like pickup popups, fail reaction overlays, and layered audio for game start, pickups, streak milestones, failure, and looping action music. Keep the design mobile-safe and easy to read, and make the tone feel tense, slick, mischievous, and instantly replayable, with the main fantasy being a vent-raiding impostor surviving longer and longer against an escalating patrol swarm.
