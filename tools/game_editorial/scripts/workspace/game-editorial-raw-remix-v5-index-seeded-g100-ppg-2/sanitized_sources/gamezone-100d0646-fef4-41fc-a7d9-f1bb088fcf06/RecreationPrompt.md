Create a compact browser game called **“Monday Mode: The Story”** that plays as a humorous branching interactive story about surviving an awful Monday. The entire experience should happen on a single polished screen with a futuristic neon-glass / arcade presentation: a dramatic start panel, a central story card, a floating emoji portrait that changes by scene, animated background particles on a canvas, visible **Energy** and **Mood** bars at the top, and a stylish ending screen when the run is over.

The gameplay loop should be simple and fast: the player presses **Start Story**, reads a short scenario shown with a typewriter-text effect, then chooses between **two large buttons** representing different reactions or decisions. Every choice should immediately change Energy and/or Mood, give punchy audiovisual feedback, and lead to the next authored scene. The scenarios should cover a full Monday arc: waking up to an alarm, deciding whether to snooze, getting ready, commuting, arriving at work, reacting to embarrassing or stressful office situations, choosing lunch, then handling the afternoon. The player goal is to reach a satisfying ending without letting either Energy or Mood hit zero.

Treat the two meters as lightweight survival resources. Bad or self-destructive decisions should drain one or both stats; smart, calm, kind, or balanced decisions should usually help. If Energy reaches zero, trigger an exhaustion-style ending. If Mood reaches zero, trigger a hopelessness or meltdown ending. Also include several authored endings with different tones, such as being fired, barely surviving the day, becoming the office hero, spending the day loafing at home, or having an introspective escape-from-work ending. Make these endings feel comedic and replayable rather than tragic.

The controls should be fully mouse-and-touch friendly: no movement, no dragging, no timing challenge, just reading and tapping choices. Add juicy feedback to every interaction: click sounds, warning and success sounds, looping background music, little particles from the button that was pressed, light screen shake on negative outcomes, and brief success/fail reaction visuals. Keep the writing concise, funny, relatable, and full of modern workplace stress energy. The tone should be colorful, sarcastic, and approachable, like a casual replayable story toy with multiple branches rather than a long visual novel.

Important constraints: keep the structure authored and branching, not open world; keep each node short and readable on mobile; make replaying for different endings a key part of the design; and make sure the UI clearly communicates that this is a Monday-survival comedy with visible stats, immediate consequence feedback, and a restart button for instant new runs.
