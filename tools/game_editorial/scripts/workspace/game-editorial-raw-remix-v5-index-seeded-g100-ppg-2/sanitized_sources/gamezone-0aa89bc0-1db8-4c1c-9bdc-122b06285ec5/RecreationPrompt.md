Create a faithful recreation of a casual arcade web game called **Skyline Snack Dash**. The game should be a full-screen, responsive HTML5 canvas endless runner with a bright cartoon presentation and very simple controls. The player controls a cute scarf-wearing dog that stays near the left side of the screen while the environment scrolls toward it from right to left. The only gameplay action is jumping: the player taps/clicks anywhere in the play area, or presses the spacebar, to make the dog leap, but only when it is on the ground. The goal is to survive as long as possible and collect floating snack or bone pickups for points while avoiding ground hazards such as crates or hydrant-like obstacles that cause an instant game over on contact.

Structure the gameplay as an endless score chase rather than a level-based game. Start with a title screen, then transition into active play with a visible score HUD, and show a game-over overlay with the final score and a restart button after a collision. Build the core loop around reading incoming hazards, timing single jumps, collecting airborne pickups placed at varying heights, and landing safely to prepare for the next obstacle. Each collected snack should increment the score and slightly increase the game speed so the run becomes more hectic over time. Use forgiving collision tuning so the game feels fair and mobile-friendly, but keep failure immediate once the player clearly clips an obstacle.

The presentation should feel cheerful, polished, and arcade-like. Draw a layered sky gradient, a warm sun glow, drifting clouds, a stylized skyline of buildings with lit windows, a dark road band, and a grassy ground strip. The dog should be animated in a lively, readable way, with some body bounce or slight rotation while airborne. Pickups should bob or spin, and collection moments should trigger particles and a score pulse effect. Add a small celebratory visual burst for successful pickups and a humorous fail reaction for game over, plus a subtle screen shake on collision. Include looping upbeat background music and short sound effects for jump, landing, pickup, fail, and UI actions. Use bold display typography, rounded glassy menu panels, and big tap-friendly buttons so the game reads clearly on both desktop and mobile. Keep the tone playful, colorful, and easy to understand at a glance, with the whole experience centered on one-button jump timing, collectible greed, and increasing runner speed.
