Create a fullscreen mobile-friendly HTML5 canvas game called "Particle Forge Command," presented as a sleek neon sci-fi control console over a dark starfield grid. The core loop should be an expressive particle sandbox: when the player touches or clicks and drags on the canvas, the game continuously spawns many small glowing particles near the pointer, effectively letting the player paint a swarm into existence. Every particle should be identical in mass and size, move with slight random initial velocity, attract every other particle with simplified n-body gravity, slow down through mild friction, bounce off screen edges, and collide elastically with each other so the swarm forms chaotic clusters and rippling chains. Show a top HUD with a live score/count display such as "Swarm Signal" and "Spark Drones," plus controls to cycle the spawn color through a bright neon palette, pause the simulation or set it to 1x, 2x, and 3x speed, scatter the whole swarm with a random impulse burst, reset the field back to empty, and open a settings panel. The settings panel should toggle graphics quality levels and audio on/off. Visually, emphasize glowing particles with radial halos, pulsing outlines, subtle crosshair highlights, short-range connection lines between nearby particles on high visual settings, and a drag reticle around the active spawn point. Add responsive mobile-safe layout, haptic feedback when possible, looping synth/action background music, and short sound effects for spawning, collisions, resets, and UI clicks. Include brief animated feedback overlays for strong impacts and resets. The tone should feel hypnotic, tactile, and satisfying rather than goal-driven: there is no level progression or win state, only the joy of spawning, watching, scattering, pausing, recoloring, resetting, and rebuilding a luminous star-swarm.
