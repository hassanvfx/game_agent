Create a mobile-first arcade driving game called **Night Shift Rush** set on a neon-lit multi-lane highway viewed from a top-down perspective. The player is working a dangerous night courier shift and controls a car with large on-screen touch buttons for **left, right, gas, brake, drift/handbrake, and NOS turbo**. The gameplay loop should be endless survival driving through dense traffic: accelerate, weave through AI vehicles, avoid direct collisions, and stay alive as long as possible while pushing for speed. Reward distance with steady cash earnings, and add a signature **near-miss mechanic** where narrowly grazing past traffic at high speed without crashing gives a large cash bonus and restores part of the turbo meter. Include a **turbo system** that temporarily boosts acceleration and top speed while draining a regenerating boost bar, and make the **drift/handbrake** increase turning responsiveness at the cost of speed, leaving skid marks as visual feedback. When the player crashes, trigger flashy effects and then show a polished summary screen with distance traveled, money earned in the run, and total saved funds, plus buttons to start another run or open a **garage**. The garage should feature **six cars** with increasing prices and speed multipliers; bought cars stay unlocked via local save data and can be equipped for later runs. Present the game with a glossy neon HUD, speed readout, distance display, money counter, boost meter, energetic gameplay music, separate garage music, responsive sound effects for crashes, near misses, turbo, and UI clicks, plus light haptic feedback if available. Rotate the environment palette over distance using different highway biomes and skyline colors to create visual progression without changing the core loop. Keep the overall tone **fast, tense, flashy, and arcade-focused**, emphasizing responsive controls, risk-reward traffic weaving, and a stylish cyber-night racing atmosphere rather than realistic simulation.
