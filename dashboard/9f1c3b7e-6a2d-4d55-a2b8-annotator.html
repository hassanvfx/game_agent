Welcome

Quick guide to the review flow.

This tool is mobile-first and local-first. Start in Discover, filter by mechanics, tap any game card to open the fullscreen playable review modal, then use Collect to save it into Favorites or your own collections.

Use More filters to enable secondary filter families such as mood, setting, characters, or semantic tags. Your collections, notes, and filter preferences are stored in local storage and can be exported or imported as JSON.
